# Expansion E3: Breaker to 200

25 Oct 2026. Breaker `brkRev 9` raises the game to 200 (`ROUND_MAX`, docs/scoring-200.md) with a bigger world and
the agreed skill features. Rooms, practice, the daily and challenges deal the new stamp; every older stamp keeps
its own step, board and scoring (proved by `expansion-e3-check`). (E3 was planned with Rally `rallyRev 9` too;
that half is not shipped here, and Rally stays exactly as on main.)

## More world

A **420 × 784** court (was 390 × 730). Joe's iPhone 11 home-screen play box is 414 × 772, and 390 × 730 already
filled it, so a taller world alone would have left bars down the sides; 420 × 784 keeps the box's shape
(784 / 420 ≈ 772 / 414) and grows the world about 8 % each way, so it still fills the screen edge to edge and the
ball travels further. On a 390 × 844 phone it sits as the old court did (390 wide, ~40 px spare below).
Every drawn object keeps its world size, so on the phone they are about 1.5 % smaller than before.

9 columns × 12 rows (108 places; 105 wall bricks and 3 gold), the paddle 60 px above the new bottom. Power-ups
(one each of laser, ×3, ×2 and Fireball), 3-hit and 2-hit bricks and the two sliding rows (rows 4–9, from 30 s and
60 s) are drawn on the bigger wall from a stream of the seed's own. The ball runs 15 % faster (4.6 px a tick,
capped at 8.6; `brkDeepSpeedFp`) so a strong player gets as far through the bigger wall in the 90 s as before (the
near-perfect bot breaks ~92 of 105, against ~74 of 80 on brkRev 8).

## Features

- **Gold bricks** (`BRK_V9_GOLD` = 3, rows 0–2, one in each third, each behind a 3-hit armour brick). Only a
  *direct hit* breaks one: a plain ball that has touched no brick since it left the paddle. Fireball balls, the
  laser (the beam stops at a gold brick), the ×3 shots and any ball that has touched a brick bounce off (a short
  white ring and a clink). Gold bricks are not part of the wall: clearing the other 105 ends the run. They glint
  while a ball is on a direct line. 15 skill points each.
- **No-touch combo**: wall bricks a ball destroys between paddle touches build a chain; ×2 from the 10th brick,
  ×3 from the 20th; a ×2 brick scores 1 combo point and a ×3 brick 3 (`brkComboPoints`), up to 40. Long chains are
  the skill, so they pay most. A paddle touch or a lost ball ends the chain; Fireball, laser and ×3 bricks never
  count (and do not end it). HUD: "COMBO ×2 · 9" above the paddle, a "COMBO ×2" pop when it steps up, "+10 ×2" on
  each combo brick.
- Kept: armoured bricks, Fireball (2.5 s), laser (4 bricks), ×3 shots, ×2 balls, sliding rows.
- Score: the wall on `BRK_V9_BAND_CURVE` (to 140), up to 10 for a quick clear, plus skill (gold + combo) up to 50.

## Tuning (bots, 60 room seeds × 4 levels, every run through the server's encode + replay)

`bun scripts/arcade-skill-bands.ts breaker --rev=x --seeds=60`: "old" is the revision rooms dealt before (brkRev 8,
LIVE_BANDS' `strictRev`), "new" the expansion (LIVE_BANDS' `rev`). Median / top 10 % (p90) / best, and the
median's change.

| Level | Old (max 150) | New (max 200) | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average | 78 / 139 / 143 | 76 / 158 / 186 | −3 % |
| Good | 107 / 139 / 146 | 113 / 185 / 191 | +6 % |
| Great | 120 / 140 / 144 | 140 / 187 / 197 | +17 % |
| Near-perfect | 139 / 146 / 150 | 167 / 189 / 200 | +20 % |

**Feature play adds on top of base play** (25 Oct, after review): the first tuning had the near-perfect bot's
median fall (139 → 120), because using the features cost more base points than they returned. The near-perfect
bot with its feature play switched off, then on (same seeds): base / skill / total 132 / 25 / 158 off, 133 / 37 /
167 on.

What changed to get there:
- **The bigger wall plays faster** (15 %), so strong play gets through as much of the wall as before, and the
  curve no longer flattens where strong players finish (the first fit put 79 bricks at 107 points).
- **Skill pays at a strong level**: gold bricks 15 (was 10); combos start later (×2 from the 10th brick, ×3 from
  the 20th) and pay 1 / 3 (was 1 / 2), up to 40 (was 30), so incidental short chains pay little.
- **The curve is fitted on the totals**: its knots were searched so each level's median total (base + skill +
  finish) lands on its target: average and good at their old medians, near-perfect at ~170.

Results against the rule:
- Good players within ±10 %: +6 % (average −3 %).
- A near-perfect player using the features lands at 167 (target 165–175).
- 190–200 only on the best runs: 10 of 240 runs reach 190+, 4 of them near-perfect; 200 itself once.
- The top 10 % of the average and good bots is wide (158 / 185): a lucky tunnel (the ball breaking bricks above
  the wall for a long while) fills much of the skill pool in one run. Capping combos lower (30) was tried and
  mostly cost the near-perfect level (167 → 162) without narrowing it, so it stays at 40. Worth watching in the
  beta data.
- Bot: `scripts/arcade-skill-bands/bots/breaker.ts` (the zone look-ahead also values skill points, weighted by
  level).

## Intro and help

Intro (three lines, `{max}` for the top): "Gold bricks: break only on a direct hit." / "Combo ×2, ×3: bricks with
no paddle touch." Help lines give the detail and the skill points. The intro still was redrawn for the new
features. **The intro demo is out of date and needs re-recording on brkRev 9**; until then
`introDemo.functions.ts` stops any demo recorded on an older revision, so the intro shows its still.

## Sounds (existing set only)

Gold brick broken → `breaker.fireballLit` (coin chime, a little higher); a bounce off gold → `breaker.wallBounce`
(thock, higher); combo steps to ×2 / ×3 → `breaker.powerUp` (pickup). All go through the sound system, so mute and
the Effects slider apply; reduced motion keeps the glint still.

## Performance

arcade-lowend-check measures only its five listed heavy games, so Breaker was measured with the tool it uses
(`bun scripts/arcade-perf.ts breaker --rates=1,4`) against its 30 fps floor: 60 fps at 1×; at 4× CPU slowdown
55.8 fps at 390 × 844 and 49.1 fps at 414 × 896 DPR 2 (main before the expansion: 44.1 there).
