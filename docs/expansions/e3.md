# Expansion E3: Rally and Breaker to 200

25 Oct 2026. Rally `rallyRev 9` and Breaker `brkRev 9` raise both games to 200 (`ROUND_MAX`, docs/scoring-200.md)
with a bigger world and the agreed skill features. Rooms, practice, the daily and challenges deal the new stamps;
every older stamp keeps its own step, board and scoring (proved by `expansion-e3-check`).

## More world (both games)

A **420 × 784** court for both (was 390 × 730). Joe's iPhone 11 home-screen play box is 414 × 772, and 390 × 730
already filled it, so a taller world alone would have left bars down the sides; 420 × 784 keeps the box's shape
(784 / 420 ≈ 772 / 414) and grows the world about 8 % each way, so it still fills the screen edge to edge and the
ball travels further. On a 390 × 844 phone it sits as the old court did (390 wide, ~40 px spare below).
Every drawn object keeps its world size, so on the phone they are about 1.5 % smaller than before.

- **Breaker**: 9 columns × 12 rows (108 places; 105 wall bricks and 3 gold), the paddle 60 px above the new
  bottom. Power-ups (one each of laser, ×3, ×2 and Fireball), 3-hit and 2-hit bricks and the two sliding rows
  (rows 4–9, from 30 s and 60 s) are drawn on the bigger wall from a stream of the seed's own. The ball runs 15 %
  faster (4.6 px a tick, capped at 8.6; `brkDeepSpeedFp`) so a strong player gets as far through the bigger wall
  in the 90 s as before (the near-perfect bot breaks ~92 of 105, against ~74 of 80 on brkRev 8).
- **Rally**: the paddle 60 px above the new bottom (a 672 px rally, was 618), gold balls, pads and both bumpers
  drawn across the wider court.

## Breaker features

- **Gold bricks** (`BRK_V9_GOLD` = 3, rows 0–2, one in each third, each behind a 3-hit armour brick). Only a
  *direct hit* breaks one: a plain ball that has touched no brick since it left the paddle. Fireball balls, the
  laser (the beam stops at a gold brick), the ×3 shots and any ball that has touched a brick bounce off (a short
  white ring and a clink). Gold bricks are not part of the wall: clearing the other 105 ends the run. They glint
  while a ball is on a direct line. 15 skill points each.
- **No-touch combo**: wall bricks a ball destroys between paddle touches build a chain; ×2 from the 10th brick,
  ×3 from the 20th; a ×2 brick scores 1 combo point and a ×3 brick 3 (`brkComboPoints`), up to 40. Long chains are
  the skill, so they pay most. A paddle touch or a lost ball ends
  the chain; Fireball, laser and ×3 bricks never count (and do not end it). HUD: "COMBO ×2 · 9" above the paddle,
  a "COMBO ×2" pop when it steps up, "+10 ×2" on each combo brick.
- Kept: armoured bricks, Fireball (2.5 s), laser (4 bricks), ×3 shots, ×2 balls, sliding rows.
- Score: the wall on `BRK_V9_BAND_CURVE` (to 140), up to 10 for a quick clear, plus skill (gold + combo) up to 50.

## Rally features

- **Moving targets**: the two pink pads glide along their own half of the far wall at 0.3–0.7 px a tick (seeded
  per spot), turning at the ends; a strike sends a pad to a new spot, still gliding. Faint rails show each half,
  two chevrons trail a moving pad.
- **Multi-ball pad**: a teal "×2 BALL" pad on the far wall from 8 s (it blinks for a second first). The blue or a
  gold ball hitting it releases a teal twin ball for 10 s (a ring round it empties as it runs out). While the twin
  and the blue ball are both up every return counts double: 2 towards the streak and 1 skill point ("RETURNS ×2").
  Losing the twin costs nothing; the pad comes back 10 s after the twin has gone, at its next seeded spot. Twin
  returns don't shrink the paddle or speed the rally up.
- **Spin shots**: the paddle's movement at contact (smoothed over the last few ticks; none under 2 px a tick, so a
  gentle sweep is enough and costs little control) gives the return a spin that bends its flight and decays. A
  spun gold ball that strikes a pink pad scores the spin bonus (10); two dots circle a spun ball the way it bends.
- **Rally milestones**: every 10 returns in a row flashes the court's wall lights gold (every other light lit) and
  scores more the longer the streak: the k-th milestone of one unbroken streak scores 2k (2 at 10 in a row, 4 at
  20, 6 at 30…); ten pips under the paddle show the way to the next one. A missed gold ball resets the count (the
  blue ball ends the run anyway; the twin never resets it). The old "RALLY 10" banner on every tenth blue return is
  replaced by the milestone banner on this revision.
- Score: gold hits (5) and pads (6) on `RALLY_V9_BAND_CURVE` (to 150), plus skill up to 50.

## Tuning (bots, 60 room seeds × 4 levels, every run through the server's encode + replay)

`bun scripts/arcade-skill-bands.ts rally breaker --rev=x --seeds=60`: "old" is the revision rooms dealt before
(rallyRev 8, brkRev 8), "new" the expansion. Median / top 10 % (p90) / best, and the median's change.

| Game | Level | Old (max 150) | New (max 200) | Median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rally | Average | 78 / 143 / 146 | 77 / 115 / 197 | −1 % |
| Rally | Good | 101 / 145 / 148 | 102 / 163 / 180 | +1 % |
| Rally | Great | 103 / 145 / 150 | 148 / 188 / 200 | +44 % |
| Rally | Near-perfect | 143 / 146 / 150 | 167 / 193 / 200 | +17 % |
| Breaker | Average | 78 / 139 / 143 | 76 / 158 / 186 | −3 % |
| Breaker | Good | 107 / 139 / 146 | 113 / 185 / 191 | +6 % |
| Breaker | Great | 120 / 140 / 144 | 140 / 187 / 197 | +17 % |
| Breaker | Near-perfect | 139 / 146 / 150 | 167 / 189 / 200 | +20 % |

**Feature play adds on top of base play** (25 Oct, after review): the first tuning had the near-perfect bots'
medians fall (Rally 143 → 116, Breaker 139 → 120), because using the features cost more base points than they
returned. The near-perfect bot with its feature play switched off, then on (same seeds):

| Game | Features off: base / skill / total | Features on: base / skill / total |
|---|---|---|
| Rally | 134 / 15 / 151 | 128 / 49 / 167 |
| Breaker | 132 / 25 / 158 | 133 / 37 / 167 |

What changed to get there:
- **Rally spin is cheap and pays**: spin needs only 2 px a tick of paddle movement at contact (was 4), so a short,
  gentle sweep through the ball spins it without risking the catch; a spin target pays 10 (was 5).
- **Rally milestones reward long streaks**: 2, 4, 6… for the 1st, 2nd, 3rd milestone of one streak (was 4 each),
  so the casual 10-in-a-row pays little and long streaks pay most.
- **Breaker's bigger wall plays faster** (15 %), so strong play gets through as much of the wall as before, and the
  curve no longer flattens where strong players finish (the first fit put 79 bricks at 107 points).
- **Breaker's skill pays at a strong level**: gold bricks 15 (was 10); combos start later (×2 from the 10th
  brick, ×3 from the 20th) and pay 1 / 3 (was 1 / 2), up to 40 (was 30), so incidental short chains pay little.
- **Bots lead the gliding pads**: the Rally bot now aims a steered gold ball at where the pink pad will be when
  the return reaches the far wall, as a strong player does with a moving target. Aiming at where the pad *was*
  cost the near-perfect bot (which steers 85 % of gold balls) far more than the good one (30 %), which is why the
  good bot out-scored the near-perfect one on units in the first tuning.
- **Curves fitted on the totals**: both curves were refitted by searching their knots so each level's median
  total (base + skill + finish) lands on its target: average and good at their old medians, near-perfect at ~170.

Results against the rule:
- Good players within ±10 %: Rally +1 %, Breaker +6 % (average −1 % / −3 %).
- A near-perfect player using the features lands at 167 in both (target 165–175).
- 190–200 only on the best runs: 15 of 240 Rally runs and 10 of 240 Breaker runs reach 190+, 10 and 4 of them
  near-perfect; 200 itself 5 and 1 times. The great level sits between good and near-perfect (148 / 140).
- The top 10 % of the average and good bots is wide (Breaker 158 / 185, Rally 115 / 163): a lucky Breaker
  tunnel (the ball breaking bricks above the wall for a long while) or a long Rally streak fills much of the skill
  pool in one run. Capping combos lower (30) was tried and mostly cost the near-perfect level (167 → 162) without
  narrowing it, so it stays at 40. Worth watching in the beta data.
- Bots: `scripts/arcade-skill-bands/bots/rally.ts` (twin ball chased after gold; better players lead the gliding
  pads, aim the blue ball at the lit multi-ball pad and sweep through a steered gold ball for spin when the blue
  one isn't landing soon) and `breaker.ts` (the zone look-ahead also values skill points, weighted by level).

## Sounds (existing set only)

Breaker: gold brick broken → `breaker.fireballLit` (coin chime, a little higher); a bounce off gold →
`breaker.wallBounce` (thock, higher); combo steps to ×2 / ×3 → `breaker.powerUp` (pickup). Rally: twin released →
`rally.bumperIn` (appear); a twin return → `rally.hit` (blip, higher); spin target → `rally.goldHit` (note,
higher); milestones sound through the shared success layer's streak banner. All go through the sound system, so
mute and the Effects slider apply; reduced motion keeps the glint, swirls and court-light flash still.
