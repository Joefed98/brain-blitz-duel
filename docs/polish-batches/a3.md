# Polish batch A3: Swish, Flightpath, Slice, Coral Pop, Gemstone, Ball Tracker

25 Oct 2026. Arcade worlds, "same game, enhanced". **Display only**: no change to sims, scoring, rules, timing,
difficulty, seeds, hitboxes, physics, server calls, data, routes, storage keys or version stamps
(`bun scripts/practice-stamps.ts` writes no change). World sizes and playfields are unchanged; new art is
painted into the existing cached layers or sprites where it is static, and stays inside each colliding object's
own outline (the 2 px bounds rule). Everything new is static except what was already animated, so reduced motion
and the Effects slider behave as before (Coral Pop's new drifting specks stop under both; Ball Tracker's marble
shadows and bin glows are skipped in reduced effects). No accessible name, test id or HUD text changed.

Files: `fairground.renderer.ts`, `binTossArt.renderer.ts`, `bullseye.renderer.ts`, `slice.renderer.ts`,
`bubble.renderer.ts`, `matchup.renderer.ts`, `ballTrackArt.renderer.ts`, and in `arenaFills.renderer.ts` only the
bodies of `sliceFill`, `bubbleFill` and `matchupFill` (each game's own screen fill; that file holds one function
per game, so other batches touching their own fills should merge cleanly). No new CSS was needed.

The game icons for Swish, Flightpath and Ball Tracker are drawn by the same painters (`drawRubberBall`,
`paintBoard` / `drawDart`, `btMarble`), so they pick up the shine too.

## Swish (`bintoss`) and Flightpath (`bullseye`): the shared fairground

`FAIR_*` colour constants, which keep their values), `src/lib/arcade/binTossArt.renderer.ts`,
`src/lib/arcade/bullseye.renderer.ts`.

  horizon and a low line of hills.
- The Ferris wheel: same place and size; a dusky frame on its A-frame stand, 12 spokes, eight gondolas with lit
  windows, 24 lit bulbs (gold and pink, each with a soft halo) round the rim and a glowing hub.
- Tents: striped tops (muted plum and violet), shaded, each with a glowing doorway, a pool of light on the ground
  and a pennant.
- Bunting: the same sagging line, flags more opaque with a lit hem and a shaded tip, a small glowing bulb between
  each pair.
- The awning: each stripe folds (sheen and shaded edges) and a gold trim runs along the hem; the twinkling bulbs are
  unchanged (still frozen under reduced motion). The canopy still ends at world y 10.
- The fairground floor (the scene below the counter's front and kick board; static, painted once per size): dark
  trodden earth with mottled patches and a lighter trodden path, two iron lamp posts near the sides with glowing
  lanterns, halos and elliptical pools of light (only where the floor is tall enough; shorter screens keep just the
  pools), and scattered confetti in the bunting colours over sawdust.
- Swish: the bucket keeps its shape and size; a sunset reflection on its far edge, a dark band under the rim, and a
  warm glow on the rim (a halo within 2 px and a lit front lip). Soft contact shadows on the counter under the bucket
  (under the trolley from throw 4) and under the ball (smaller and fainter the higher it flies). The ball gains a warm
  rim light and a second soft sheen, inside its radius.
- Flightpath: the board keeps every ring radius and value colour; the wood grain through the paint is fainter (clean
  rings), the ring wires brighter, a varnish sheen across the upper left, a bevelled rim and a stronger warm halo
  behind it. The hay bale has shaded ends, three layers of straw and loose strands along its lit top. The dart is
  polished steel and chrome with gold flights; each dart keeps its own colour as a stripe down the flights.
- Kept: the wind arrows and bars, the pennant, the HUD lines, the throw dots, the aim lines, balloons, hoops and
  spinners exactly as they were.

  `seaWater`: a little sunlit teal near the top, near-black navy at depth.
- **Light rays**: a Coral Pop–only `bubbleRays` (five additive rays, a bit brighter than the shared `seaRays`, which
  Scrumper still uses unchanged). Off under reduced effects; still under reduced motion (seaClock is 0).

- **Bubbles**: `bubbleGloss`, painted into the cached sprites of every colour bubble and special: a fine dark outer rim,

  bound). The icons are unchanged.
- **Floor**: a sandy floor 26 world px deep under the gun (a lit wavy crest, ripples, grains, pebbles, two shells). On it:
  pink and coral-red coral fans and two brain-coral domes either side of the gun, and a far-reef silhouette behind. Two
  short swaying kelp fronds were added on the sand, beside the margin kelp. Everything is below the shooter line and
  clear of the gun and its magazine (x 160–300 of 390).
- **Screen fill** (`bubbleFill` in arenaFills.renderer.ts, that function only): it now starts from the world's sand
  colour at its foot (`#6E5A3E`, exported as `BUB_SAND_FOOT`), so there's no seam. Below that: sand ripples, grains,

- Unchanged: the launcher, queue, aim trail, the dashed danger (shooter) line and the HUD.

Assumptions: the dashed shooter line is the "danger line". Background reef art may sit in open water as long as it
stays below that line (the field can never get there).
