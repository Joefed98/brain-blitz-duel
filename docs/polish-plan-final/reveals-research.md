## 3. The fit problem

### 3.1 What `useRevealFit` does (`AnswerReveal.tsx:333-516`)

1. On layout effect, resets any earlier transform, then — unless the card is empty or still `[data-reveal-waiting]` — finds **boards** with `revealBoards(card)` (l.275-317): anything with `data-reveal-board`, a canvas/svg/img ≥ 56 px tall, a ≥ 56 px css `grid` whose text is all ≤ 3-character runs (`cellText` l.277), or a box made only of absolutely placed children. Each leaf is then widened to tight single-child wrappers (≤ 40 px larger) l.300-314. **Heuristic**: a word grid (Lookout 10×10 letters) qualifies; a chip list or a `TileRow` does not; the number kit's `.nrv-board` qualifies by attribute, so its tabs stay unscaled but its note text inside scales.
2. Measures the root's visible box (`REVEAL_CARD_PAD` 12 l.251), the **lines** (every element with own text outside the boards) and each board's painted/clipping **holders** (l.416-429).
3. `apply(z)` l.440: `transform-origin: top center; transform: scale(z); margin-bottom: margin + (z − 1) × h` — i.e. height is compensated by a margin, **width is not** (a grown board stays centred by its origin but its layout box is unchanged, so neighbours and the tray see the old width).
4. `fits()` l.453: the drawn box's bottom ≤ visible bottom, every board inside the side pads, no new board–board or board–line overlap, no board escaping a holder it was inside at 1×.
5. Direction l.466-485: if 1× fits, search upward to `REVEAL_GROW_MAX` 1.8 (l.249); else downward to `REVEAL_FIT_MIN` 0.5 (l.247). Eight bisections; |z − 1| ≤ 0.02 → 1. Sets `data-grown` / `data-fit` on the scroller.
6. `placeTray` l.367 positions the absolute `.reveal-tray` on the union of `boardBox(card)` (`src/lib/boardBox.ts`) and the boards.
7. Re-runs on `ResizeObserver` (root, card, card children) and a `MutationObserver` on `hidden`, `data-solution-switch`, `data-solution-view` (l.499-508), keyed by `${w}x${h}:${card.scrollHeight}` l.492.

### 3.2 Why it misfits

- **Text scales with the board.** The whole board element (letters, clue numbers, labels inside it) is transformed. At 0.5× a 13 px Mosaic clue is 6.5 px; at 1.8× a Slider 26 px letter is 47 px while the lines round it stay 13–15 px. The design intent ("the text keeps its size", l.330) only holds for text *outside* the board.
- **Canvases are bitmaps.** Minefield's `ArcadeRunSummary` canvas, Trail/number svg… an svg scales cleanly but a canvas grown to 1.8× is blurry (drawn at world px × DPR ≤ 2, then CSS-scaled). Nothing re-renders at the fitted size.
- **Width is not compensated.** A grown board's layout box keeps its 1× width, so the flex column centres the *old* box; `fits()` only guards against escaping the 12 px pads. Lines under a grown board are pushed down by `margin-bottom`, but any element beside it (two-column pairs that the switch didn't hide: Mosaic, Pattern games before `data-solution-view` applies) can be overlapped horizontally — `fits()` catches board–board overlap and refuses to grow, so these reveals stay small instead.
- **Two sizing systems fight.** Pattern boards (`pipes`, `laser`, `flow`, `push`, `unblock`, `gravity`) size themselves from `cqh` of a `flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 0` container that sits inside `.reveal-card` (`flex: 1 1 auto`) inside `.answer-reveal-content` (`flex-1 min-h-0 overflow-y-auto`). The container's height is whatever flex leaves, which depends on the lines above it, the chart/players row below, the hero spot (64 px when earned), and the practice action row — then `useRevealFit` applies a transform on top. Trail (`trail.tsx:466-485`), Integer Pyramid (`numberPyramid.tsx:628-640`) and Checkout (`checkout.tsx:982`, `window.innerHeight`) compute cells from hand-subtracted budgets ("−150 px", "−56 −54 −34 −64") that encode today's shared layout; a redesign of the shared rows silently breaks them. Four games reach out of their card with `closest(".answer-reveal-content, [data-reveal-screen]")` (`iceSlide.tsx:382`, `pipes.tsx:548`, `laser.tsx:1028`, `push.tsx:278`).
- **Fixed-px boards** assume a ~340 px budget: Interlock 7 × 40 (`tileGrid.tsx:457`), Slider 5 × 52 (`wordSlide.tsx:484`), Roundabout `min(340/cols, 300/rows, 40)` (`letterWheel.tsx:598`), Nucleus 330 (`wordsmith.tsx:82`), Tally 340 × 300 and 260 (`countUp.tsx:427-430`), Counterweight's svg `width`, Eagle Eye cells, Safe Cracker dials, Ascend discs. At 375 × 667 the card's inner width is 375 − 2×12 (px-3) − 2×12 (card pad) = **327 px**, so 340-wide boards always trigger the shrink path (0.96 → and everything inside shrinks).
- **`vw`-based boards** (`min(84vw, 320/340px)` in Forage, Lookout, Cryptogrid, Swapword) read the *viewport*, not the column — fine on phones, but on desktop (560 column) they hit the 340 cap and the grow then scales them 1.6×, enlarging letters past the lines' type.
- **Short phones (375 × 667, `SHORT_REVEAL_QUERY`)**: after header (≈36), verdict row (≈60), score line + best (≈40, inline payoff adds ≈30), players row (≈30) and practice buttons (44 + gaps), the scroller has ≈ 350 px; a square board plus a label, caption, key and chips (the Pattern/Logic kits add 100–150 px of lines) cannot fit at 1×, so the fit halves the board (and its text) or the card scrolls under the fade mask (`styles.css:1209`) with the tray cut off. `styles.css:1197` also forces every `.text-xs/.text-sm` inside the card to 1rem, defeating the kits' 12–13 px captions.
- **Desktop (1440 × 900)**: the column is 560 × 1000 but the viewport is 900 tall, so the reveal is ≈ 860 px tall; boards grow up to 1.8× (Slider cells → 94 px, letters 47 px; Trail's fixed cells stay ≤ 56 because they are computed, then transformed anyway), canvases blur, and the 60 px arcade score with a 380 px-wide cards picture floats in a 560 column. `data-grown` is reported but nothing uses it.
- **Arcade path has no fit at all**: `useRevealFit` gets null refs (the `solvedBoard` card only exists in the puzzle branch). `[data-reveal-column]` just scrolls (l.325-327), the chart refuses to shrink (no `min-h-0`), and in rooms the Strategy pictures and Home Run / Top Bins pictures are **hidden** behind "See breakdown" during a 5–6 s reveal.
- **Likely mis-fitted today** (from the sizing above, not from screenshots): at 375 × 667 — Tally (340 × 300 field), Interlock/Slider pairs before the switch applies (first frame), Mosaic (two boards side by side, 12 px clues → ~6 px when halved), Roundabout (340 × 300 crossword), Counterweight (fixed svg), Integer Pyramid and Trail (budget arithmetic for a layout that has since changed), Pipeline/Lightbender/Chroma Lines/Heave (cqh collapse + transform), Minefield (canvas scaled), every Cards & Board reveal (tall canvas → scroll). At 390 × 844 — the same at smaller scale factors; Mosaic and Tally still shrink. On desktop — every DOM/canvas board grown to 1.8× (Slider, Interlock, Cryptogrid, Swapword, Minefield, Kakuro) while captions stay 13 px; arcade reveals a narrow 380 px picture in a 560 column.
