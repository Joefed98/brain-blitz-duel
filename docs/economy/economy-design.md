# Joulz – Economy design: shop, Season 1 pass and coins

Nov 9, 2026 · @Joe

The economy is fully designed, which finishes wave 2's design. Agreed with Joe on 9 November 2026; every number is a starting value to tune with beta data.

## Summary

- **One currency, JLZ coins,** never earned from normal play. Every new account gets 1,000, enough for exactly one shop Epic.
- **The shop sells Epic, Elite and Legendary only:** 1,000, 1,600 and 2,800 coins. Common and Rare items are earned, never sold.
- **Four coin packs,** better value as they grow: 500 for £0.99, 1,000 for £1.79, 3,000 for £4.99, 10,000 for £14.99.
- **52 items at launch,** four a day in the daily shop, plus a themed bundle every Monday. Wishlist hearts instead of countdowns.
- **Joules fill the pass:** 1,000 per level, 60 levels, with a soft cap of 4,000 a day.
- **Season 1 "Neon Circuit" pass:** a free track of 30 rewards ending in two Legendaries, and a premium track of 60 rewards (£4.99 or Joulz Plus). 40 items, all exclusive forever.
- **Three weekly challenges** every Monday: 750, 1,000 and 1,500 Joules (500, 750 and 1,250 until the Joules rework of 13 Nov 2026).

**Changes from the master plan**

- Five rarities replace four. Common and Rare leave the shop.
- Shop prices move from 100 / 300 / 900 / 2,000 to Epic 1,000, Elite 1,600, Legendary 2,800.
- The four old coin packs (500 for £0.99 up to 13,000 for £19.99) are replaced by the four packs above.
- Crimson, Royal and Emerald card arts move from Rare to Epic.
- World themes leave the launch shop; four appear in the Season 1 pass instead.

The legal adviser confirms this meets UK and EU expectations for in-game currency.

## The launch shop: 52 items

The shop opens with 52 items (the 51 below plus the Frost card art from the Frozen Lake bundle), mostly Epic so most daily slots are affordable. Art follows the rarity ladder: Rare plain and simple, Epic a step up, Elite emphatic, Legendary bold and colourful, often with gold, and always animated (a gentle shimmer or movement suited to the art, stopping under Reduce Motion). A few items here and there also get an unusual finish, so they stand out and players want them (a style palette of metallic, pixel, halftone, papercut, neon tube, retro sticker, art deco, holographic, static and glitch), while most stay in the main Joulz style. Names and art beyond the card arts are drawn and approved on the canvas first, as with the badges.

**Not in the launch shop:** world themes (wait for Season 2, so new players learn the original looks first); badges and titles (always earned, never sold); gem colours (mastery stars only); season peak-tier rewards and tournament exclusives (never sold).

## Daily rotation, featured set and bundles

**The daily shop** shows four items, changing at midnight UK time, the same for everyone.

| Slot | Rarity |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Epic |
| 2 | Epic |
| 3 | Elite |
| 4 | Epic, Elite or Legendary, with a Legendary on about two days a week |

- No item repeats within 7 days. Each Legendary returns roughly every three to four weeks.
- Items a player owns are skipped for them where possible, otherwise shown as "Owned".
- Two Epic slots every day mean a new player can always spend the 1,000-coin sign-up reward.

**The weekly featured set** changes every Monday, the same day the weekly challenges reset: one themed bundle (a game's skin pack, or a matching card art, frame and name colour), cycling through the three sizes. Its items can also be bought one by one that week at full price.

**Bundles** are priced in coins with 20–30% off the items bought separately, with the real-money value shown.

| Size | About | Coins | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | £6 | about 3,100 (22.5% off; 3,400 would break the 20–30% rule, corrected 11 Nov) | Four matching Epics (4,000 separately) |
| Medium | £10 | about 5,500 | A game-skin pack mixing Epic and Elite |
| Large | £20 | 10,000 | A full themed set with a Legendary centrepiece; one Mega Pack covers it |

**Fair selling, especially to under-18s**

- No flashing countdowns: a quiet line, "New items at midnight".
- No "only 3 left" or fake scarcity. Every shop item comes back.
- A **wishlist heart** on any shop item; when it returns, the player gets an in-app notice.
- No paid random boxes, ever.
- When a player can't afford a tournament entry, a plain "Get coins" button sits beside the price. No pop-ups, no pressure.

## Joules and pacing

Every level takes a flat 1,000 Joules, so the 60-level pass needs 60,000. Joules come from the Dailies, rated matches, tournaments, the weekly challenges, and stars and challenges in Practice, win or lose.

**The Joules rework (Joe, 13 Nov 2026).** Practice games (20 each, up to 200 a day) and rooms (20 a round, 50 for finishing) no longer pay Joules. Mastery stars pay once each, the Dailies went from 150 to 200 each and the weekly challenges from 500 / 750 / 1,250 to 750 / 1,000 / 1,500, so a player keeps about the same pace through the season (table below). Joules already credited are never changed. Retired games count towards nothing: their stars and Practice challenges pay no Joules and their plays count towards no weekly challenge (the catalogue's retired list, so a game brought back pays again).

| Activity | Joules | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rated match, best of 3 | 150 for a win, 100 for a loss | None |
| Rated match, best of 5 or 7, or 4 or 8 players | 1.5 times the above | None |
| Daily Puzzle | 200 | Once a day |
| Daily Arcade | 200 | Once a day |
| Friend challenge | 100 | First 5 a day. A challenge's creator is paid for their own attempt only once another player has finished it |
| Tournament: an Arena duel | 100 | None |
| Tournament: finishing a Score Attack, Swiss or Knockout | 150 | Per event |
| Mastery star (Classic) | 150 | Once per star, ever. Easy 50 and Hard 250 are provisional, to confirm in phase 3: until then those stars pay nothing |
| Practice challenge, first completion | 100 | Once per challenge, on every level |
| Weekly challenges | 750, 1,000, 1,500 | Three a week (see below) |
| Room, Practice game | Nothing (20 a round plus 50 for finishing, and 20 a game up to 200 a day, until 13 Nov 2026) | – |

Stars and challenges earned before a season starts never pay (Joules count from the season's start for everyone). Nothing pays for a run the server flagged or for a Daily archive replay.

**Soft cap:** after 4,000 Joules in a day, further play earns half. Nobody is ever stopped from playing.

**Expected pace over the 8-week season** (the Joules rework, 13 Nov 2026; `bun scripts/joules-pacing.ts` plays each profile day by day on the pass's own arithmetic)

| Player | Typical play | Season 1, before → now | Season 2 | Season 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 3 days a week: both Dailies, 5 Practice games a day, a room and 2 rated matches a week, the Easy weekly 6 weeks in 8; 12 challenges and 6 stars a season | Level 17 → 18 | 17 → 17 | 16 → 17 |
| Regular | 5 days a week: both Dailies, 10 Practice games, 3 rated matches a day, 2 rooms and 2 friend challenges a week, Easy and Medium weekly every week and Hard 3 weeks; 40 challenges and 20 stars | 58 → 59 | 56 → 56 | 55 → 54 |
| Keen | Every day: both Dailies, 10 Practice games, 8 rated matches, 4 rooms, 5 friend challenges, 4 Arena duels and 2 Score Attacks a week, every weekly; 100 challenges and 50 stars | 60 in week 4 → week 4 | week 4 → week 4 | week 4 → week 5 |

Season 2 and 3 are a returning player with fewer stars and challenges left (Regular 10 and 25, then 6 and 15). Phase 3's Easy and Hard stars and Hard and Expert challenges refill that pot; Easy 50 and Hard 250 are set so a returning player's one-off Joules stay about Season 1's.

Joules stay secondary: a small line on results in electric lime (#C6FF3D) with the bolt mark, never shown alongside ratings.

- None need friends.

- Season 1 uses 8 weeks from this pool of 45; the rest are spares for tuning.

## Coin amounts

Coins are never earned from normal play: no coins for match wins, streaks or pass levels beyond the coin rewards listed. These are the only free sources.

| Source | Amount | How often |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-up reward (accounts only) | 1,000 | Once |
| Free pass track | 1,000 | Per season |
| Premium pass track | 1,500 | Per season |
| Legendary badges | 250 each | Once per badge (2,000 for all eight) |
| Practice challenges: hardest Classic and Hard ones | 100 | Once per challenge |
| Practice challenges: Expert ones | 250 | Once per challenge |
| Rewarded ads, chosen by the player | 20 | Up to 5 a day |
| Tournament prizes | Fixed per event, never pooled from entries | Decided in the wave 4 design chat |

The 39 coin-paying practice challenges (17 Classic and 13 Hard at 100, nine Expert at 250) are worth about 5,000 coins over a player's whole time with Joulz (5,250 exactly), as one-offs that can't be farmed. 1,700 of it can be earned before difficulty levels arrive in wave 3.5. Reviewed with the catalogue on 10 November.

**What an engaged free player earns in one season:** free pass 1,000, about ten coin challenges 1,000, a couple of ads most days about 2,200; **about 4,000–4,500 coins in all.** That's a shop Epic every couple of weeks or one Legendary a season, with some left for tournament entries.

**Ads are never personalised for anyone,** matching the privacy decision of no ad tracking and the stricter rules for under-18s. The legal adviser confirms this alongside the privacy policy.

Coins can never be cashed out, traded or gifted.

## Joulz Plus

Joulz Plus is the membership: £3.99 a month or £34.99 a year, so Plus never costs less per season than the premium pass alone (yearly works out at about £5.38 a season against the pass's £4.99, and saves about 27% against paying monthly) (agreed 9–10 November). Screens on the canvas board "Joulz Plus".

| Perk | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Premium pass | Every season's premium track, included |
| Coins | 200 every month |
| Streak freezes | 2 every month |
| Full stats and rating history | Every season's rating graph with peaks; a page per category; per-game stats; every match ever; head-to-head against every opponent. The free profile keeps everything the master plan promised (this season's graph, category trend lines, last 20 matches) |
| Weekly report and drills | What improved, where you lose points, and a one-tap drill playlist of 3–5 games with target scores |
| Replay review | Watch any of your runs back, with the moments that cost points marked |
| Ghost races | In Practice, race the ghost of a friend's best or a top player's run (always labelled). Racing your own best is free for everyone |
| Daily archive | Play any past Daily Puzzle or Daily Arcade, unrated |
| Early access | New games in Practice a week before everyone else |
| Plus mark | An optional gold "PLUS" chip on your profile, off by default |

**Never in Plus:** anything that affects scores, ratings or matchmaking; Plus-only modes or rooms; Plus-only badges or titles; bonus Joules.

**Later (from Season 2):** a monthly Plus-exclusive cosmetic. **Founding Supporter** (£14.99, December to 25 January) stays as planned: a year of Plus for early backers.

**Build notes:** ghost races and replay review reuse the server's stored replays and the shared round engine; the privacy policy gains a line on ghosts. Stats depth arrives with ratings in wave 3; the weekly report and drills follow once there is enough match history.

## Notes for the wave 2 build sessions

- **One catalogue table** holds every item with its kind, rarity, source (shop, pass free, pass premium, earned, tournament) and an exclusive flag. Pass items are flagged so the shop rotation can never pick them.
- **The coin ledger is server-only:** every change is a recorded line, never an edited balance; pack purchases credit only after the server checks the receipt; refunds reverse through the ledger.
- **Joules, levels and claims are server-side and idempotent;** the soft cap and per-activity limits are counted per UK day.
- **The skin hook, once per game:** the seven skinnable games (Free Cell, Full House, Crazy Golf, Cue Ball, Top Bins, Apex, Curl Champ) read colours from a theme object. World themes for Apex, Curl Champ, Cue Ball and Crazy Golf extend the same hook to backgrounds and scenery, with no change to layout or hit areas.
- **One automated test per skinned game** replays a run in every skin and world theme and checks the score is identical; every skin passes the contrast check.
- **The rotation** is chosen by the server once a day, the same for everyone, with the slot rules and the 7-day no-repeat rule above. Wishlist returns raise an in-app notice.
- **Weekly challenges** are picked from the pool each Monday, with at least one not needing rated play. Before wave 3, challenges marked R are left out of the draw.
- Coin prices only on cards; the real-money value at the Starter pack's rate shows on the purchase confirmation.
  - **Navigation (agreed 10 Nov):** a fifth bottom tab, Shop: Play · Practice · Tournaments · Shop · Profile. Inside it, a Shop · Pass · Plus switch at the top (Shop keeps Featured, Daily and Coins). The coin counter opens Shop › Coins; the pass keeps its progress card on Home. No badge or dot on the Shop tab except when a wishlisted item returns. Every screen with the bottom bar moves to five tabs.
- **Coin packs and the premium pass** use a stand-in until Stripe and store purchases exist.
- **Analytics events** for wave 2: shop viewed, item bought (coins, item, rarity), pack bought, pass bought, pass level reached, reward claimed, weekly challenge completed, wishlist added.

**Still to do before the wave 2 prompts:** none. All 52 shop items and 40 pass items are drawn on the canvas (boards: Card art and frames, Game skins, World themes, Avatar finishes, reactions, celebrations, tiles), and Joulz Plus is agreed above.
