# Expansion E6: Coral Pop, Gemstone, Ball Tracker to 200

25 Oct 2026. Gameplay change on three arcade games, each raised from 150 to 200 through `ROUND_MAX`
(docs/scoring-200.md). New rounds deal `bubbleRev 8`, `matchupRev 9` and `btRev 7`; every older revision
replays and scores exactly as before (e6-check replays 48 runs recorded on main before the change).

Scoring shape (all three): **base points** on a refitted curve over the game's own units (to 150 with the
finish bonus, as before) **plus a feature bonus** of up to 50 from the new skill features only. The base
curves were refitted on the bigger worlds so today's average / good / great players land where they did
(±10 %); only strong use of the features reaches 190–200.

## Coral Pop, bubbleRev 8 (`src/lib/arcade/bubble.ts`, `bubble.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/bubble.tsx`)

- **More world**: the reef starts 10 rows deep (95 bubbles, was 67) in a 20-row field, in the same 390 × 730
  tank. The danger line moves down to just above the gun (y 630, was 543), so the reef has the same 263 px
  (nine descents) to fall as before; gun, shot speed, queue, Jokers, mine or torpedo and descent pace unchanged.
- **Treasure chests** (`BUB_CHEST`): six seeded chests in rows 3–9, never touching. A chest is no colour
  (nothing matches it, a Joker ignores it); it pays only when it falls with a cluster (`dropLoose`). A mine or
  torpedo that destroys one directly pays nothing. Art: the same glass bubble round a small wooden chest with
  brass bands and a gold glint; a chest tally on the rock HUD; "TREASURE" rises where one falls (chime).
- **Bank shots**: a shot that comes off a wall at least once and removes bubbles ("BANK", zap).
- **Combos**: every removing shot straight after another one ("COMBO ×n" in the HUD and over the reef; tick, a
  ding from the fourth).
- Feature points: chest 5, bank 1, combo 1 → `BUB_V8_FEATURE_CURVE` → 0–50.
- Base: bubbles cleared (chests included) of 95 on `BUB_V8_BAND_CURVE` (140 from 89), a clear adds up to 10 for speed.

## Gemstone, matchupRev 9 (`src/lib/arcade/matchup.ts`, `matchup.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/matchup.tsx`)

- **More world**: seven columns of the same 50 px cells, ten rows (was eight), in a 390 × 720 world (was 560);
  the goals panel sits under the board. Gems are drawn exactly as before. Play is matchupRev 8's: a swap resolves
  its whole cascade and the board waits for the gems to fall.
- **Gem goals per level**: each level asks for two colours (three from level 3) and a count of each (8, then two
  more per level); every gem of a goal colour cleared counts. A level met starts the next at once ("GOAL!", chime).
- **Frozen tiles**: ice on board cells, seeded per level in rows 4–9 (four, then one more per level). The gem in
  an iced cell can't be swapped; clearing the gem there (line, cascade or blast) breaks the ice (crackle).
- **Cascade multiplier**: each cascade step after a swap's own clear counts ×2, ×3…; the extra match points earn
  feature points (one per 40). The shared layer's "CASCADE ×n" banner shows it (zap from ×3).
- **Treasure gems** (`MATCHUP_TREASURE`): a gold medallion with a ruby, placed in row 4 of 10 whenever none is on
  the board (up to eight a run, seeded columns). It can't be matched, swapped or blasted; it falls as gems under
  it clear and is collected on the bottom row ("TREASURE!", ding).
- Feature points: level 6, ice 1, treasure 12, cascade per 40 → `MATCHUP_V9_FEATURE_CURVE` → 0–50.
- Base: match points on `MATCHUP_V9_BAND_CURVE` (150 at 3,200).

## Ball Tracker, btRev 7 (`src/lib/arcade/ballTrack.ts`, `ballTrackArt.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/ballTrack.tsx`)

- **More world**: 13 rows (was 12), 700 × 1400; 36 marbles (was 30), released every 84 ticks (was 103, last
  at 49 s). Courses from a new proved bank, `src/lib/duel/ballTrackBankV7.server.ts` (300 courses,
  `bun scripts/ball-track-bank.ts --v7`, each proved by its playbook on the btRev 7 rules). btRev 6's rules
  (tubes turn with a marble inside, switch points, re-lay, colour shuffle) unchanged.
- **Speed boosters**: three fixed straights per course with orange chevrons; a marble leaving one takes its next
  three steps at double speed (zap when one is boosted).
- **Gold marbles**: six per course (one per six marbles); a gold band inside the marble's own radius. Their cup
  is their colour's; home they are worth more (chime).
- **Combo**: every correct landing at a streak of three or more ("×n" on the queue shelf and in the status row;
  tick, a ding every fifth). A wrong cup, a lost marble or a crash ends it.
- **Crossovers**: two X tiles per course (fixed straights under a rotatable tube): a marble from above drops
  straight through, one along the row rolls straight on. Two meeting in one from different directions are both
  knocked out (crackle).
- Feature points: gold 4, boosted marble home 1, combo landing 1 → `BT_V7_FEATURE_CURVE` → 0–50.
- Base: marbles home of 36 on `BT_V7_BAND_CURVE` (140 at 36), all home adds up to 10 for speed over
  [`BT_V7_LAST_LAND_MIN` 3189, 3600] (the earliest last landing, proved by search over the bank).

## Notes and assumptions

- **Intro demos are out of date** for all three: `introDemo.functions.ts` stops each until a demo is recorded on
  the new revision (the intro shows its still). They need re-recording.
- Crossovers rarely bite in bot play (the better bots plan round an X; no crash in the measured runs); they are a
  hazard for human timing, measured by e6-check's crafted meeting.
- Gemstone's near-perfect bot tops out at 178: 190–200 needs two or three treasures and several goals in one run,
  which the bot's single-swap look-ahead rarely manages. 200 is reachable on the curves (e6-check).
- Sounds reuse the shared celebration set (`shared.celebrate.chime`, `zap`, `crackle1/2`, `countTick`,
  `countDing`) through `playSound`, so mute and the Effects slider apply; no new audio files.
- Reduced motion and reduced effects: floating words and banners hold still; booster chevrons stop cycling;
  the boosted marble's streak is skipped.
