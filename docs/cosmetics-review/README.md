13 Nov 2026, from main `f268bcd`. A review only: no app code, migration, check or fixture changed. Joe's rule of
13 Nov: Joulz is premium, so every cosmetic offered anywhere must look premium and beautiful. Anything that doesn't is
improved or withdrawn, never offered as it stands. Joe judges the look; the verdicts below are this session's honest
read, to help him decide.

- **The contact sheet:** [`index.html`](index.html). Open it from a checkout; every image path is relative. It shows
  every item at phone size (390 wide, DPR 2) and at 1440 × 900 (DPR 1), next to its approved still, with its id,

  - `render-identity.tsx` draws card art, frames, name colours, avatars, gems, finishes, reactions, celebrations, tile

  - `render-screens.ts` captures the real app (Profile, Customise's four tabs, Shop, Pass, Plus, Practice). It runs

  - `build-sheet.ts` writes the page. The verdicts live in `tools/verdicts.ts`.
  - The approved-art comparison sheets in `png/skins-vs-art/` come from `scripts/skins/compare-art.ts`.

## 3. Card fronts versus card backs

**Card backs** show only on face-down cards: Full House's deck today, and potentially Summit, Thirteen, Five Hands and
Golf Solitaire (section 5).

**Card fronts** would show on every card in every card game that uses the shared deck (`cards.renderer.ts`
`drawCard`):
- Free Cell, Summit, Five Hands and Thirteen in rooms;
- Full House and Golf Solitaire in Practice.

They wouldn't reach Mahjong Solitaire, which draws its own tiles (a separate "tile set" item would be needed there).
Fronts are far more visible than backs: a player looks at fronts for the whole round.

Three options. The mockups are on the sheet. Each starts from the app's real faces and copies the indices and pips
untouched.

| Option | What changes | Readability | Reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Card stock** (recommended) | The paper (colour, a faint weave, a foil or neon edge) and the card's outline | Unchanged: index and pip size, place, font and red/black colours untouched. The paper must stay light (contrast ≥ 7:1 with the ink, the same contrast check the skins use). | Every card of every shared-deck game. One painter in `paintFace`, through the same per-call look hook (`withCardsLook`). |
| **B. Court set** | Only the J, Q, K portraits (and perhaps the Ace of Spades' emblem) | Indices untouched; 12 of 52 cards change. Low risk. | The same games. Most visible in Free Cell and Five Hands (large cards); small cards use the large-index layout, so their portraits are small. |
| **C. Ink finish** | The suit and rank ink get a foil or metal finish; red stays red and black stays black | Risky: a gradient on small index glyphs lowers contrast at 48 px. Not recommended. | The same games |

**Never sell a four-colour deck** (a different colour per suit). It makes suits faster to read, which helps win. If
wanted, it belongs in Settings, free for everyone.

**Recommendation.**
- Start with **A**: card stocks as a new kind, three to start (Epic, Elite, Legendary).
- Add **B** court sets later.
- Drop **C**.

Requirements and how it fits:
- Fronts need the same proofs as the skins: a default-frame digest per game, recorded on main before the hook (an
  addition, not a change to `skins-default-fixture`); a contrast check on every face; and a replay check proving
  identical scores.
- A front and a back from the same set make a natural bundle.
- Card backs then stop being sold as a lone item for one practice game.

## 4. Skins closer to their approved art

Today's painter output is beside the approved art in `png/skins-vs-art/` (approved | ours | difference), and the
in-game frames are on the sheet. What I'd change, all within the bounds rule (art within 2 px of the hit area,
nothing bigger):

- **Neon and plasma ball glows** (Crazy Golf's Neon Glow and Plasma, Cue Ball's Neon Ball Set and Neon 8-ball):
  - The approved art carries an outer glow; ours draws the rim with little or no glow.
  - Draw the glow as a soft shadow inside the 2 px allowance; it can extend further only on the drawn trail, never
    the hit area.
  - Under Reduce Motion it stays still.
- **Curl Champ's stone handles** (Glacier, Aurora, Neon Stones):
  - The art shows a shaped handle (a raised arch with a highlight). Ours draws a flat bar.
  - The handle sits inside the stone's circle, so drawing it properly changes nothing that collides.
- **Neon Night** (Apex):
  - The kerbs should chase and the lights pulse as in the art; ours is slower and dimmer.
  - Speed and brightness can follow the art, through `skinTime()` (still under Reduce Motion).
- **Neon Course's sails** (Crazy Golf):
  - The art draws neon-edged sails with a lit hub. Ours draws dark sails with gold slats.
  - The sails' turning stays tied to the sim's own sails (no change to timing).
- **Pixel Pool's digits** (Cue Ball):
  - The 3 × 5 digits are drawn at fractional positions, so they blur into smudges at play size.
  - Snap them to whole device pixels at the stage's scale. The art is pixel art and should stay crisp.
- **Checks.**
  - `skins-default-check` is untouched: the default path doesn't change.
  - `skins-check` already proves contrast, bounds and Reduce Motion for every look.
  - `skins-replay-check` proves identical scores.
  - Only `compare-art.ts` (a tool, not a check) improves.

## 6. Withdrawing and improving, once Joe decides

### Withdrawing an item

**There is no "for sale" switch.**
- `public.catalogue_items` has no active or retired column.
- Its constraints tie the price to `source = 'shop'` and `exclusive` to `source <> 'shop'`
  (`20261110160000_economy.sql:103–108`).
- The shop query (`catalogue_shop`, `:132`) and `sellable()` (`src/lib/shop/dailyShop.ts:72`) select by source.

**Recommended: a new, additive migration from 20261116600000** that adds `withdrawn_at timestamptz` to
`catalogue_items`. Then:
- `catalogue_shop`, `sellable()`, `shop_record_days`, the bundle purchase and `economy_purchase_item` all refuse a
  withdrawn item;
- Customise's `sourceLine` (`src/lib/identity/customise.ts:25`) stops saying "Shop" for it.

Why not delete the row:
- Rows can't be deleted: `inventory_items`, `shop_wishlist` and the seasons table reference them without cascade.
- Stored `round_results.look` and `async_results.look` keep the ids (CLAUDE.md rule 4: never rewrite stored data).

**What else changes:**
- **Shop rotation:** recorded `shop_days` and `shop_weeks` stay as history; the next day's draw skips the item.
  Withdrawing a Legendary changes the "every 3–4 weeks" rhythm (`shop-check.ts:198`).
- **Bundles** (`src/lib/shop/bundles.ts`): every bundle holding the item needs a new item list and price (20–30 % off
  the parts), and its blurb in the file and in `docs/economy/bundles-season-1.md`.
  - The Full House felts are in **Card Table**.
  - Spotlight is in **Big Night**; Shockwave is in **Black and Gold**.
  - Captain's Calls is in **Big Night**; Retro Arcade is in **Retro Arcade**.
- **Pass rewards** (`src/lib/pass/rewards.ts`): a withdrawn level takes another reward kind. The options are 100 coins
  (`C`), a Score Attack or Category Cup ticket (`SA`, `CC`), a streak freeze (`FZ`), or a better item.
  - Neon Felt at premium 12 is best replaced by a Five Hands or Summit card stock or back, so the level stays a
    cosmetic.
  - The economy doc's track table must say the same (`pass-check.ts:175` reads it).
  - The Season 1 pass hasn't opened to anyone but testers, so nobody loses a reward they were promised.
- **Badge rewards:** only the Founding frame (`badges.server.ts:49`). It stays.
- **Starter sets:** the 8 free card arts and Plain (`economy.on_new_profile`). `economy.remove_item` refuses starters
  (`:991`). Improving them is a redraw, not a withdrawal.

**People who already own it (today mostly testers):**
- They keep it. It stays equippable and drawn.
- If an item has to go entirely, `economy.remove_item` deletes the inventory row, and the loadout slot goes with it
  (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). The card then falls back to the default (`knownLook`). Coins are only returned through
  `economy_refund` of the spend line.
- `shop.remove_test_purchase` only reverses a test coin line.
- `pass.remove_test_season_rewards` removes test-season items.
