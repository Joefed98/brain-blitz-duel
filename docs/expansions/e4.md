# Expansion E4: High Rise and Pinball (25 Oct 2026)

World expansion and depth for two arcade games, each raised from 150 to 200 through `ROUND_MAX`
(`docs/scoring-200.md`). Gameplay changes, each on a new version stamp: **High Rise `skylineRev 7`** and
**Pinball `pinRev 4`**. Every older stamp plays and scores exactly as before (proved below). Rooms, practice,
the daily and challenges deal the new stamps from this PR on.

## High Rise: `skylineRev 7` (`src/lib/arcade/skyline.ts`, `skyline.renderer.ts`)

**More world: more sky.** The camera keeps much more sky over the tower: it climbs to 60 % of the view
before the camera pulls back (half before), zooms out further (0.34 against 0.45, so the city and the
whole tower stay in view as it climbs higher), and pulls right back at every milestone. The view stays
Tuning E's 390 × 735: a taller world (tried at 390 × 770) no longer fills the iPhone 11 box's width and
leaves side strips the parallax city cannot run on into (arcade-fill-check failed on a seam), and 735 is
the tallest world that fills the width on the iPhone 11 home-screen app and a 390 × 844 phone. The sim is
horizontal only, so speeds and spacing did not need retuning; the wind from block 20 and the overhang rule
are rev 6's.

**Skill features** (in the game's Real World night style: steel and glass blocks, the gold crane, the
moon and the lit city):

- **Perfect-drop streak multiplier** (extends rev 2's streak bonus): a perfect drop scores ×2 from the 3rd
  perfect drop in a row, ×3 from the 6th and ×4 from the 10th; any other drop resets it. The HUD shows
  "Streak 7 ×3" (gold while above ×1) and "next perfect ×4" just before a tier; the PERFECT pop reads
  "PERFECT ×3"; the shared success layer's banner says "STREAK ×3" on each new tier.
- **Special blocks with a next-block preview.** From the 5th block, about 3 in 10 blocks are special
  (never two in a row), dealt from the room seed (`skylineKinds`, its own PRNG stream, the same for every
  player). A "NEXT" pill under the crane's jib shows the next block in its kind's look and names it:
  - **Gold** (gold glass, a glint sweeping across it while it slides): the usual width; landed perfectly it
    scores a bonus ("GOLD BONUS" pop, a chime).
  - **Narrow** (a red hazard band): three quarters of the top's width (never under 32 px or the top):
    harder and worth more.
  - **Wide** (a green band with outward arrows): 48 px wider than the top (capped at the start width):
    easier and worth less.
  - A narrow or wide block is perfect when its centre lines up with the top's (within the usual 3 px);
    centred, it stays whole, so a centred wide block widens the tower again. Off centre, the overhang is cut
    as always.
- **Height milestones every 10 floors:** a bonus, a gold "FLOOR 20 · CITY BONUS" banner with sparks off
  the top block, the zap sound, and the camera pulling right back for 100 ticks to show the whole tower
  and the city before easing in again.

**Scoring to 200** (`skylineV7Units`, `SKYLINE_V7_BAND_CURVE`): each block counts 5 (narrow 8, wide 3),
each perfect drop 5 times its streak multiplier, a gold block landed perfectly 15 more and every
milestone 20; the units go through a curve fitted on the skill-band bots (see the table) so each level up
to great lands near its rev 6 score and only long perfect streaks, gold blocks and milestones climb to
190–200. No input still scores 0.

## Pinball: `pinRev 4` (`src/lib/arcade/pinball4.ts`, `pinball4.renderer.ts`)

**More world.** A taller table, 390 × 820 (700 before), at the iPhone 11 box's height (so it is drawn a
little narrower, 367 px). pinRev 1's lower table (flippers, slingshots, the drop-target bank, the three
bumpers, the spinner in the left orbit, the multiplier lights) moved down 120 px unchanged, and a new
**upper playfield** above it. The slope is a touch lighter (gravity 40 against 48) so a good flip reaches
it. pinRev 4 lives in its own module and reuses pinball.ts's swept-contact primitives, which were only
exported (no code changed), so pinRev 1–3 play byte for byte as before (pinball-check, e4-expansion-check).

**Skill features** (the Retro neon table: cyan rails, magenta plastics, gold flippers and inserts):

- **J-O-U-L-Z lanes** (extends the three top lanes to five): a ball through a lane lights its letter (lit
  letters carry over from ball to ball); every fresh flipper press shifts the lit letters (left press left,
  right press right), so a dark letter can be lined up under an incoming ball. All five: a big bonus and the
  next table multiplier (the existing ×2…×5 lights).
- **Two ramps:** a ball rolling up into a ramp's mouth fast enough is a clean ramp shot: it rides the ramp
  (a fixed path and time, drawn as plastic channels over the playfield), scores, and lights the next
  multiplier (×2…×5). The left ramp climbs to the plunge rail and drops the ball onto the U lane (shift the
  letters while it rides); the right ramp crosses over and returns the ball down the left orbit, through the
  spinner, to the left flipper. A ramp shot within 4 s of the last one's exit builds a combo (+1,000 a
  step; the arrows under the mouths show the next "×2"). Too slow and the ball rolls back out of the mouth.
- **Multiball lock:** a clean ramp shot lights the lock (the ramp arrows turn gold and read LOCK); while it
  is lit, each ramp shot carries the ball up into the lock hole at the top of the right ramp, where the
  locked balls wait in a row. The first and second locks plunge a new ball (no ball lost); the third
  releases all three for 20 s with the **jackpot** lit: a magenta standup behind the drop-target bank that
  counts only when hit from below (so the targets must be down). While another ball is in play a drain
  costs nothing. The HUD shows "LOCK LIT", "2 LOCKED: MULTIBALL LIT" and "MULTIBALL 14".
- **Skill shot:** the ball waits on the plunger while a power meter in the shooter lane sweeps up and down
  (twice a second); any flipper press plunges at that power (it plunges itself after 4 s). The plunge rides
  a wire rail round the inside of the arch and drops into a lane set by the power: five equal zones of the
  meter, soft Z at the bottom to full J at the top, each lettered on the meter. One letter flashes gold (on
  the lane and the meter); plunging into it scores the skill shot.
- Kept: the spinner, bumpers, drop targets and multiplier lights, all scoring as in pinRev 1 (and the
  multiplier counts on them, as before). The expansion's awards (ramps, combos, locks, jackpots,
  J-O-U-L-Z, the skill shot) are flat, not multiplied, so one lucky multiball cannot swing a run past
  every skilled one.
- Also fixed on the new table only: pinRev 1's flat one-way gate at the top of the shooter lane could hold
  a ball (seen in bot runs); the new gate is tilted down towards the playfield, and two balls touching push
  apart (equal masses), so multiball balls never pass through each other.

**Scoring to 200** (`PIN_V4_BAND_CURVE`): table points through a curve fitted on the skill-band bots,
each level up to great near its pinRev 3 score; a near-perfect player's median rises to about 160 and only
runs with multiball jackpots, combos, J-O-U-L-Z and skill shots reach 190–200.

## Assumptions

- "Lights the next multiplier ×2–×5" uses the table's existing multiplier (the ×2…×5 lights), raised one
  step by each clean ramp shot and by J-O-U-L-Z; a drain resets it, as in pinRev 1.
- The lock is lit by a ramp shot, and lit locks are reached through the ramps (the ramps divert into the
  lock hole). Bot runs showed a free-standing hole on the upper playfield was almost never reached by real
  flips, and a weak plunge could drop into it, which would have made multiball trivial.
- The skill shot's lane is set by the plunge's power on a rail rather than by free physics: on the open
  arch the lane a plunge reached changed chaotically from one power step to the next, which no player could
  learn.
- Pinball's ramps, rail and lock hole are on a raised level: drawn over the playfield, translucent, with no
  collision (a playfield ball passes under them), as on a real table. Every solid on the playfield is drawn
  on its collision capsule (the bounds rule).
- Both games keep 60 s and the same controls (tap; hold left or right half), so desktop keys are unchanged.
