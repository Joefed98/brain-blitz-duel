# Expansion E1: Jaywalk, Hover and Rainbow Bridge to 200

25 Oct 2026. World expansion and depth (a gameplay change, stamped). Each game gets a bigger world filling the
iPhone 11 home-screen app (414×896), its agreed skill features, a new version stamp and a round maximum of 200
(`ROUND_MAX`, docs/scoring-200.md). The extra 50 comes only from the new features. Older stamps replay and score
exactly as before (each game's expand check replays runs recorded on main before the change).

| Game | New stamp | World (was) | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jaywalk (`cross`) | `crossRev 8` (`CROSS_REV_EXPAND`) | 390×727 (390×580) | 200 |
| Hover (`hover`) | `hoverRev 8` (`HOVER_REV_EXPAND`) | 390×727 (390×735) | 200 |
| Rainbow Bridge (`inkbridge`) | `inkRev 9` (`INK_REV_EXPAND`) | 390×674 (390×620) | 200 |

All three fill their 414×896 arena box edge to edge (presentation-geometry-check rewrote
`docs/arena-sizes-oct-2026.md`). The world size rides on the stored revision (renderer, reveal height, effects spec,
fills, puzzle component).

## Jaywalk, crossRev 8

- **World**: 36 px HUD, 34 px goal kerb, 14 rows of 44 px (13 traffic lanes and the island at row 7), a 28 px start
  pavement (was 34), 13 px foot. Courses from `rollCourseV8` (the old fair rules, cyclists and roadworks; never on the
  island), each proved passable over a 1,200-tick horizon. The shared helpers read a course's own lane count; older
  courses have 9 lanes, so their results are unchanged.
- **Checkpoint island**: a grass strip (clover, daisies, hedges, a flag that turns gold once reached). Reaching it
  banks the crossing's progress; a later hit in that crossing restarts the walker on the island (1 s pause).
- **Gold coins**: four per crossing, one in each of the two fastest traffic lanes either side of the island (never
  roadworks), in a seeded column near an edge (1–2 or 10–11). Standing on the cell collects it: 3 points.
- **Buses**: from crossing 2 (two lanes from crossing 5), 136–168 px long, 4/5 of the car speed, 80–112 px gaps.
- **Motorbikes**: from crossing 3 (two lanes from crossing 6), 36–43 px, the fastest car speed + 200–340 (1/256 px a
  tick), two per lane 560–1,060 px apart. Both drawn in the vehicle style (shadow, sheen, headlight glow) within 2 px
  of their boxes.
- **Streak**: each crossing without a hit raises it; multiplier min(4, streak) shown as "×N" on the HUD pill; each
  clean crossing pays 1 × (multiplier − 1); a hit resets it.
- **Score**: play part `bandPoints(CROSS_V8_BAND_CURVE, rows + 2 × crossings)`, curve
  `[0,40,50,58,66,74,82] → [0,57,68,89,113,136,150]`; bonus min(50, 3 × coins + streak points); total capped at
  `roundMaxFor("cross")`. No input scores 0.
- **Sounds** (existing): coin `arc_retro_coin`, island `arc_fair_bell`, streak `arc_fair_sting`.

## Hover, hoverRev 8

- **World**: 390×727 (`HOVER_EXPAND_H`): the height at which a 390-wide world fills the 414×772 box exactly (rev 7's
  735 left 2 px bars at the sides). The course is rev 7's (barriers, gaps, two-gap and sliding barriers, ten gold
  stars, spacing, scroll ramp), drifting over the full height; the features come from their own PRNG stream.
- **Tight gates**: five per course on plain reachable barriers: an extra 80 px gap with gold rims (the narrowest
  normal gap is 100 px), at least 56 px of wall from the main gap and 24 px from floor and ceiling, within reach of the
  neighbouring gaps. Threaded cleanly (inside on every tick the saucer overlaps the barrier, no shield break): 5 points.
- **Star chains**: five per course (3–4 on 0.3 % of courses), 5–7 stars ≥ 16 px apart along one flap's arc at that
  stretch's scroll speed, between two barriers without gold stars. Same star art, glow and "+1". A complete chain: 5
  points; a chain with a missed star fades.
- **Shield**: two cyan bubbles per course (early, mid). Held, a cyan ring within 2 px of the saucer. A crash with a
  shield breaks it and gives 45 ticks untouchable: through a barrier (it still counts), off the floor with a tap's kick,
  or a stopped climb at the ceiling. No points.
- **Score**: base (barriers ×3 + gold stars ×2 on `HOVER_V8_BAND_CURVE`, up to 140, + 10 for lasting the minute, ≤ 150)
  + bonus (25 over the tight gates + 25 over the chains a course holds, 5 each on a full course), capped at 200.
- **Sounds** (existing): `hover.star` for chain stars, gates and chains; `arc_retro_pickup` for the shield;
  `hover.crash` for a shield break.

## Rainbow Bridge, inkRev 9

Joe's brief for this game was cut off after "bounce clouds that send"; the features below are an assumption
(clouds send the coin back up).

- **World**: 390×674 (`INK_TALL_H`), carried in state and per level. inkRev 3's layouts with the pot 54 px lower;
  gravity scaled by 620/674 (59, 50 on live levels) and stored per level so the coin arrives at the old speed; ink
  9 % higher. Every level still proved by its reference line.
- **Bounce clouds**: on up to 5 of levels 1–8 (never the live levels), a 52×20 capsule. The coin is pushed out and its
  inward speed reflected (236/256 kept, at least 5 px a tick out). A cloud is kept only if the reference line still pots
  without touching it, the coin never pots unaided, and a proved trick line (a ramp, plus a catch ramp if needed)
  bounces off it and pots within the level's ink, robust to 3 px shifts. Potting after a bounce is a trick shot. Drawn
  in the shaded-cloud style with a gold edge and spring zigzag; squashes when hit (not under reduced motion).
- **Gold stars (extended)**: on most of levels 1–8 (typically 6–7 a round); on a cloud level the star sits on the trick
  line's highest point after the bounce, high in the new sky. Each star is proved on its own release.
- **Score**: base (levels on `INK_V9_BAND_CURVE`: 0, 42, 56, 71, 86, 97, 106, 116, 126, 132, 140 for 0–10 levels,
  plus up to 10 for speed; ≤ 150) + floor(50 × (stars + trick shots) / (star levels + cloud levels)), capped at 200.
- **Sounds** (existing files): `inkbridge.cloudBounce` → `cross.hop.mp3`, `inkbridge.trickShot` →
  `sfx_spark_chime.mp3`, replay `inkbridge.trick-shot` → `replay.moment.mp3`.

Bot runs through the server replay. Each cell is median / top 10 % / best.

**Rainbow Bridge** (80 seeds a level):

| Level | inkRev 8 | inkRev 9, no features | inkRev 9, features at skill rates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average | 44 / 65 / 102 | 42 / 57 / 101 | 42 / 71 / 97 |

The bots rarely clear all ten levels (nor did they on inkRev 8); a scripted perfect run reaches exactly 200 on every
tested seed and 150 without the features. Going for a trick shot risks one of the three attempts, so the "great"
bot's feature median dips.
