## E7 · Roofbound (`flip`) — flipRev 7

25 Oct 2026. Roofbound's expansion: a bigger cave, five skill features, scored to 200 (`ROUND_MAX.flip = 200`).
Rooms and practice deal `flipRev 7`; every earlier revision replays and scores exactly as before.

### World

- **Taller world: 390 × 730** (was 390 × 620; `FLIP_V7_WORLD_H`, `flipWorldHFor(rev)`). At 414 × 896 (iPhone 11
  home-screen app) the arena box is 414 × 772, so the world draws at 1.058× (412 × 772) instead of 1.06× of a
  658 px world with 114 px of rock fill underneath: it fills the screen.
- **The cave's height changes along the run** (`flipV7HeightAt`): normal 260 px for the first 8 s, then sections of
  5–8 s alternating between tight (200 px), normal (260 px) and tall (340 px), each change eased over 160 px of
  tunnel, the cave centred on y = 365. **A flip's arc follows the height** it leaves at (`flipV7Arc`): 9 ticks in a
  tight stretch, 12 at normal, 16 in a tall one, so flips change in distance and timing.
- **Longer runs, more rooftops**: the top speed is 2,560 (was 2,304; still +90 every 3 s) and two **speed zones**
  (5 s each, starting between 17–20 s and 37–40 s) run at 11/8 speed, so a full run covers about 16 % more tunnel
  (more hazards, more roofs in the far city, which now draws a second, further row of rooftops). The course's x at
  every tick is a table (`v7.xs`) built with the course.
- The bend (from 10 s, the rev-4 table) is unchanged and still shared by both surfaces; hazards ride it.

### Features (all deterministic, integer sim, gated on the stamp)

| Feature | Rule | Points (of the extra 50) |
|---|---|---|
| **Star trails** | Four trails (about 7, 20, 33 and 48 s), each five small stars on a flip's arc from a launch chevron on one surface. A star is taken in the air when the runner is within 24 px of it along the tunnel and within 23 % of the cave height of it across (`flipV7Frac`, the art's own ease). The course gives each trail a clear stretch and is proved with every trail and gold star on one route. | 5 per full trail (20) |
| **Double-flip combos** | A flip started within 8 ticks of landing with a hazard under the runner or within 96 px ahead counts; chained ones count more (double 1, triple 2, longer 3), twice in a speed zone. A crash save ends the chain. | combo points, capped at 15 |
| **Moving spikes** | From 12 s, a quarter of the decision patterns are a spike sliding 40–70 px to and fro along its surface (period 1.5–2.5 s). Collision follows the slide exactly (`flipSegShift`); the art draws the spike there over a dashed groove marking its whole sweep. | (a hazard) |
| **Speed zones** | 11/8 speed for 5 s; warm air, chevrons and a gate at each end. | 3 per zone run through without a save (6) |
| **Checkpoints** | Gates at 15, 30 and 45 s. Passing one readies a save (a gold shield in the tunnel's corner): the next crash is saved — a second of shield (60 ticks), the combo lost, and the survival count paused for 10 s, so lasting on never scores less than crashing there. Without a save a crash ends the run as before. | 3 per checkpoint reached with no crash so far (9) |

### Scoring (`flipV7Score`, `flipV7Parts`)

- **Base** (the rev-6 shape): units = half seconds survived (the paused ticks left out) + 3 per gold star, on
  `FLIP_V7_BAND_CURVE` (units 0/40/105/130/140/146/147/150 → 0/10/40/56/72/84/98/140), plus 10 for lasting the minute.
  So the base still tops out at 150 only for the full minute with all ten gold stars.
- **Features**: trails + combo + zones + checkpoints, capped at 50.
- **Total**: base + features, capped at 200; no input scores 0. The reveal's lines add up to it (`bandLines`).
- The base curve is lower than rev 6's strict curve below the top because the features add on top: fitted so the
  bots' medians land within about ±10 % of rev 6's (table below).

## E7 · Grappler (`swing`): world expansion and depth, swingRev 12

25 Oct 2026. Gameplay change on a new stamp, **swingRev 12** (`SWING_REV_EXPAND`). Rooms deal it from
`SWING_V12_BANK`; every earlier revision plays and scores exactly as before. The round's maximum is
**200** (`ROUND_MAX.swing`).

### What changed

- **More world**: the view is 450 × 844 (was 390 × 730), the shape of the iPhone 11 arena box
  (414 × 772), so the whole box fills and more cave shows ahead (the hero sits 225 px from the left).
  The ceiling, anchors, ceiling spikes, stalactites, dragon and every course draw are rev 9's. The lava
  is 224 px below rev 8's (114 below rev 9's), each lava jet's top where it was. The ten touch stars
  are re-proved on the deeper cave.
- **High and low routes**: 12 cave crystals (gems) sit at the tops of proved flights near the ceiling
  (the high route; touch radius 12 px). 8 speed gates hang under anchors, their openings around the
  deepest proved fast swing (the low route: grab early for a long rope and swoop through at
  7 px a tick or faster; the gate lights gold). Neither can end a run.
- **Swing combos**: a flying grab of an anchor further ahead at 7 px a tick or faster, within 60 ticks
  of the last grab, adds a link; every fifth link is a combo point. A slow grab, a grab from a ledge or
  a landing ends the chain. Natural play rarely chains (strong releases do): see the tuning table.
- **Course sections (set routes)**: two or three per course before the dragon (from about 22, 80 and
  145 m): a wide start ledge (where most open flights come down), three landing ledges and a
  finish-flag ledge, hung from the roof on chains. Ledges are one-way: falling on to one from above,
  off the rope, lands the hero, who walks to its centre (6 px a tick; a press on the way is held until
  there) and kicks off with the next press (a push along the swing). Each ledge sits where a proved
  kick-and-release from the ledge before lands, with releases a tick or two either side landing too.
  **Collapsing ledges**: the middle landing ledge of sections 2 and 3 cracks and gives way 45 ticks
  after the first landing. Every ledge landed counts once.
- **Score** (`swingScoreV12`): a base curve on metres + 5 a star (as every band revision; 140 at
  500 m with all stars, + 10 for 500 m), refitted to the deeper cave, plus feature points capped at
  50: combo points (up to 25), a point a gem (12), a point a lit gate (8) and 15 shared by the ledges
  landed (1 each with three sections, 1.5 with two). 60 available, 50 counted, so 200 needs nearly
  everything. The reveal's breakdown lists each part.

### Assumptions and decisions

- "Fills the screen": the world widens as well as deepens (an even taller 390-wide world would leave
  side bars). Everything draws about 8 % smaller on an iPhone 11 than rev 11.
- The "low route" is the long-rope swoop through the gates under the same anchors; no second line of
  anchors (a nearest-anchor grab would steal grabs from the main line and break its proofs).
- Course sections are added to the existing proved course (ledges, no anchors moved); a player can
  always ignore them and swing through.
- The base curve was fitted by matching the bot players' unit quantiles on rev 12 to their curve points
  on rev 11 on the same bank courses (30 a level), then the low end lifted 5 points so average players
  land as today on room courses: `SWING_V12_BAND_CURVE` units [0, 55, 74, 129, 166, 215, 260, 329, 489,
  515, 550] → [0, 46, 60, 84, 93, 107, 117, 129, 133, 135, 140].
- Init cost: the sections, gems and gates are found by simulation at init (cached per seed): median
  ~170 ms, worst ~430 ms on the server here; the phone does it once a round.
- The reveal box stays 390 wide; a rev-12 reveal draws at 390 × 731 (the world's shape).
- Sounds: the existing set through the shared success layer (pop, streak, perfect cues). No new files.
- Reduced motion: the gate glow, flag ripple, ledge shake and falling chips hold still; reduced effects
  skip the gate band, gem glow and ledge underside light. Nothing that collides or scores is skipped.
- Art on the sim's geometry: ledge top = landing line, slab width = landing reach; gem drawn at its
  touch radius; gate posts flank the opening (the gate is a line test, not a body). Hero unchanged.

## E7 · Orbit: world expansion (orbitRev 11, 25 Oct 2026)

Orbit's part of expansion session E7. Rooms and practice now deal **orbitRev 11**; every older revision replays
and scores exactly as before. Round maximum 200 (`ROUND_MAX.orbit`).

### What changed

- **More world.** The camera shows a 450 × 782 slice of space instead of 390 × 650 (about 40 % more area, the
  iPhone 11 home-screen box's own aspect, so the world fills the 414 × 720 arena). Usually one or two more
  planets are in view, and the course reads further ahead. The view rides in the state (`viewW`, `worldH`);
  the camera (`orbitCamera.ts`) frames the next planet 360 px from the bottom on rev 11.
- **Course.** Rev 11 keeps the proved 40-planet bank courses and black holes (rev 10's play). Rooms deal one of the
  371 bank courses that carry proved features (`src/lib/duel/orbitXpBank.server.ts`, built by
  `bun scripts/orbit-xp-bank.ts` with `scripts/orbit-xp-lib.ts`). Assumption: "more planets" means more planets in
  view, not a longer course. A longer course would need a new proved planet and hole bank, and would push the sun
  past the 90 s clock for most players.
- **Skill features.** They are drawn in the A4 Orbit style (sim geometry, same palette, glows skipped with reduced
  effects, still with reduced motion):
  - *Stardust trails*: 5 trails of 6 gold motes on a hop's best line. The rocket collects a mote it touches
    (10 px rocket plus 4 px mote).
  - *Slingshot moons* (gravity assist): 4 small moons, each with a dashed teal ring 44 px round it. A flight that
    passes through the ring and leaves it without touching the moon is flung on at 1.5× speed. Touching the moon
    (8 px body) is a crash.
  - *Asteroid fields*: 3 lines of rocks across a hop (6–7 px rocks, crash like belts, use the checkpoint), with a
    dotted gap line. Crossing the gap's line threads the field.
  - *Perfect orbits*: letting go during the first lap round a planet and landing (or entering a hole). A gold arc
    shows the rest of that first lap.
  - *Black holes* are unchanged.
- **Scoring.** The base is rev 10's score: the strict curve on planets reached (written out as
  `ORBIT_V11_BAND_CURVE`, the same knots), plus up to 10 for reaching the sun quickly; 150 at most. The bonus is
  1 per 2 motes (15), 4 per slingshot (16), 4 per field threaded (12) and 1 per 3 perfect orbits (7), so 50 at
  most. The total is capped at 200, and no input scores 0.
- **Proof.** Every bank entry stores a witness tap log that reaches the sun with every hole and feature taken and
  scores 200 through the server replay (orbit-rev11-check replays all 371). Each feature is placed on the
  witness release's own line and kept only if that release still lands and the hop keeps a 4-tick window.
- **Sounds** come from the existing set: a tick per mote, a zap per slingshot, a chime per threaded field and a
  ding per perfect orbit (`gameSounds.ts` orbitSounds). They follow mute and the Effects slider. No new files.
- **Texts.** The intro HOW lines are now "Tap to let go; the next orbit catches you." / "Grab stardust; sling
  through moon rings." / "Thread rock gaps; let go on the first lap." The checkpoint and black-hole lines moved
  to the help. SCORE: "Planets and the sun, plus stardust, slingshots, fields and perfect orbits: up to {max}."
  The help explains each feature and its points. The intro still is updated.
- **Intro demo: needs re-recording.** Only a demo recorded on orbitRev 11 plays (`introDemo.functions.ts`), so
  the intro shows its still until Joe records one.
