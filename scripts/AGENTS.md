# Scripts

- Colour Flood: the routine check (scripts/logic-grid-check.ts) quick-tests every bank board and runs the exhaustive par proof on a seeded sample of 40; the full proof (scripts/flood-par-proof.ts) runs only at bank rebuild; why: proving all 400 is too slow for a routine run.
- Puzzle banks (flow, blockparty, tubes, gravity, lightup, iceslide): the routine check (scripts/puzzle-bank-check.ts) verifies every entry and rebuilds a sample from its fixed seed; the byte-for-byte rebuild of all six (scripts/puzzle-bank-rebuild-check.ts, ~20 min) is optional; why: a full rebuild is far over the 300 s check limit.
- Cryptogrid (codeword) draws from a script-built, server-only bank of 1,000 boards (scripts/codeword-bank.ts, rebuilds byte-for-byte; `--check` compares); scripts/codeword-check.ts proves every board has exactly one solution against the full dictionary; why: answers stay off the phone and uniqueness is proven offline.
- Strikeout, Cryptogrid and Swapword answers come only from src/lib/duel/commonWords.server.ts, built by scripts/common-words-build.ts from SCOWL 2020.12.07 sizes 10–35 (needs the SCOWL tarball; the list is committed); why: answers are everyday words while typed words still use the full dictionary.
- Drawing checks that run without a real canvas use the shared stub context in scripts/canvas-stub.ts (canvasStub, CANVAS_STUB_METHODS); a renderer that starts using a new value-returning canvas method (a gradient, measureText, getTransform…) gets it added there once; why: hand-rolled per-check stubs fell behind the renderers (Tuning D's createRadialGradient broke ink-rev3-fix-check).
