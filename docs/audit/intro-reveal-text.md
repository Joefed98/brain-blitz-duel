# Intro, How to play and reveal text: every active game (audit, Nov 2026)

Generated by `bun scripts/audit/intro-reveal-text.ts` from the live modules (92 active games, registry order within each section). Nothing in the app was changed. Screenshots: `screenshots/audit-nov-2026/`.

Where each text lives:

- **Intro** (`src/components/duel/RoundIntro.tsx` → `PuzzleInstructions`, `src/components/duel/PuzzleInstructions.tsx`): GOAL `instructions.goal`; HOW the lines of `instructions.how` without `helpOnly` ("**action** detail"); SCORE `INTRO_SCORE[type]` (`src/lib/duel/introScore.ts`, `{max}` filled from `roundMax.ts`); then the definition's `introLine` (`src/puzzles/<id>.tsx`) when it has one, and "You have N seconds" (the limit, less 3 s on an arcade clock).
- **How to play** (the "?" help, `PuzzleInstructions` with `overlay`): GOAL; every HOW line, `helpOnly` included; SCORE `instructions.score` (`src/lib/duel/instructions.ts`, `PUZZLE_INSTRUCTIONS`).
- **Puzzle reveal** (`src/components/duel/AnswerReveal.tsx`): the verdict line `verdictLine()` — "Solved in N.N s" / "Solved" / "Partly solved" / "Not solved" ("Scoring…" while grading); under it `revealHeading` (default "The answer was"); the stored score; a near-miss line (`src/lib/duel/nearMiss.ts`, some games); then the definition's `Reveal` component (`src/puzzles/<id>.tsx`) with the stored score parts (`scorePartsText`, `src/lib/duel/puzzleScoreRev.ts`: "150 + 11 moves + 15 speed", "N for progress").
- **Arcade reveal** (`src/components/duel/ArcadeReveal.tsx`, lines built on the server by `buildArcadeReveal` in `src/lib/arcade/reveal.ts`): on the arcade scoring standard one line per part, `"<label>: <detail> → <points> of <max>"`, then `"Total → N of 200"` (or `"At least 1 for playing → 1"`; `stdLines`, `src/lib/arcade/arcadeStandard.ts`), folded to one line until tapped: `"N · core X + features Y"` (`revealSummary`); plus stat tiles and the score chart.
- **Shown after the audit run**: the reveal's visible text after the practice run that made `-reveal.png` (scripted or driver input; see `screenshots/audit-nov-2026/capture-report.json`). Lines are as `innerText` reads them, including header and button labels.

- HOW: **From maze 3, two marbles,** each to its own cup.

**How to play ("?")**

- GOAL: Roll each marble into its own cup.
- HOW: **Drag left or right** to spin the maze.
- HOW: **Keys open gates;** maze 4: small and tight.
- HOW: **From maze 3, two marbles,** each to its own cup.
- HOW (help only): **Marbles always roll down.** 4 mazes, always all on screen: three big ones, then a smaller, tighter one with the blue marble alone and no holes. A marble that falls into a black hole goes back to its start: it costs time and ends your clean run.
- HOW (help only): **Keys and gates:** a gate across the way to the cup stays shut until either marble rolls over its key, which waits in a side passage before the gate. A gate and its key share a colour.
- HOW (help only): **Ice:** on the faintly frosted cells the marble keeps its speed and slides further, so ease off before a turn or a hole.
- HOW (help only): **Cups:** the blue marble goes in the blue cup, the amber marble in the amber cup, which always starts well away from it. Each marble can only leave through the gap by its own cup, and the maze is done when both are in. The marbles roll through each other.
