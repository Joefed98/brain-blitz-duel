# Joulz badges

54 permanent badges, earned never bought (see the Badge catalogue doc for how each is earned).

- Shape = group: shield (competitive), 8-point star (mastery), serrated seal (Daily and tournaments), hexagon (challenges), rounded diamond (fun), crest (social and special).
- Rarity = finish, on the five-tier ladder used across Joulz (badges and shop): Common bronze, Rare silver, Epic gold, Elite obsidian, Legendary diamond-plated. Icons and numbers are engraved.
- The 11 category masters: Logic, Pattern, Speed, Attention, Word, Number, Action, Retro, Sports, Cards and Board, Strategy.
- Mix: 8 Common, 12 Rare, 17 Epic, 9 Elite, 8 Legendary. Legendary badges pay 250 coins. Players show up to three on their card.

`svg/` has every badge; `badges.json` lists slug, name, group, rarity and coins; `source/badges2.py` draws them.
