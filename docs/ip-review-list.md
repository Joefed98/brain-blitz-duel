# IP review list (for the solicitor, master plan step 23)

Names, looks and sounds to put to the solicitor's freedom-to-operate review before the store launch. Each entry says
what the game is close to and what we did to keep clear of it. Not legal advice: the review decides.

| Game (working name) | Id | Close to | What we did | Added |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blockfall | `blockfall` | Tetris (falling-block line-clear games are a genre; the name, look and music are The Tetris Company's) | Our own name (never "Tetris" or "tetromino" in the app, code or docs we wrote for it); our own block style (glass tiles with a neon rim and corner glint) and our own colours in Retro's cyan family, not the classic per-shape colours; no Tetris music (its bed is our own Fission Retro track); the shapes, the 10 × 20 well, hold, a ghost and three-piece preview are common to the genre. | 1 Nov 2026 |
| Alley Roll | `alleyroll` | Skee-Ball (a registered trade mark for the ramp-and-rings alley game) | Our own name (never "Skee-Ball"); our own art (a warm wooden alley, a lit ring board and a scoreboard drawn in code); generic ring values 10–50 with two 100 pockets; library sounds only. | 1 Nov 2026 |
