# The arcade 200 expansions (26–27 Oct 2026): Hover, Grappler and Roofbound

Four parked arcade expansions were finished under the arcade rule, each with one new precision feature: Hover (from
#165), Grappler and Roofbound (from #164) and Scrumper (from #163). **Hover (`hoverRev 9`), Grappler (`swingRev 13`)
and Roofbound (`flipRev 8`) ship.** Scrumper (`snipRev 11`) is left out (below; parked on `claude/x200-snip-parked`).
Per-game notes: `x200-hover.md`, `x200-swing.md`, `x200-flip.md`, `x200-snip.md` (on the parked branch).

## The bar (Joe, 27 Oct, replacing every earlier number target)

The game is fun and its scoring fair, consistent and appropriate; a brilliant player scores more than a great one,
who scores more than a good one; anyone who actually plays scores above 0 (a run with no input may score 0); an
ordinary (good) player's score doesn't jump wildly from before. 200 need not be reachable on every seed; precise
tuning will come from real beta scores.

## The arcade rule

1. **Same run for everyone, seeded opportunities**: every feature and pickup appears at seeded moments and places,
   never from how a player has played. `arcade-room-schedule-check` plays every room deal of each shipped game idle,
   at random and as the weak, good and elite players and fails unless every scheduled item (`scripts/x200/<type>.ts`)
   reads the same in every run, the opportunity counts agree, and the styles really played differently.
2. **Skill headroom**: features pay a modest amount on a rising scale on top of base scoring; full value needs
   elite play.

## Measurement

`bun scripts/arcade-skill-bands.ts <type> --rev=x --levels=weak,average,good,great,near,elite,perfect,random` on
#175's fixed bots (`docs/arcade-bands-elite.md`), each level on the same seeds (paired). "Before" is the old stamp
(the game's `strictRev`) played by the same fixed bots. Tuning seeds n 0–59, fresh n 10000–10199 (measured once).
`perfect` is the flawless level (no human error), `random` the bot module's `randomBot`. Each bot plays its game's
old stamps exactly as #175 has it and adds the new stamp's feature play.

## What changed after #175's bots (27 Oct)

- **Hover**: 1 base unit once the saucer has flown 0.5 s or reached the first barrier (a run with no input still
  scores 0); centre-line grades pay 4 / 2 / 1; full skill pay at 58 raw of 70 and base 140 with every barrier and 8
  of 10 stars, so a flawless human-paced run can lose one slip at each end. A flawless bot
  (`bots/hoverFlawless.ts`: the 260 px on screen, a beam search re-planned every 6 ticks, at most 10 taps a second).
- **Grappler**: combos pay in tiers at 5, 10 and 15 fast regrabs (2, 3 and 5 points; 25+ regrab chains are gone);
  par points 25; skill pay full from 46 raw; base 140 at 500 m with three stars; the bank 168 courses (ledges never
  under a stalactite; a section's last hop never under one).
- **Roofbound**: no preview strip (Joe's decision): the runner sits further left on flipRev 8 (x 50, was 90; display
  only, the sim's collision never reads it), so 340 px of course show ahead, and the course builder places gold stars
  and sweet spots only where a settled runner can still take them when they come on screen and a reaction time
  later; sweet spots one landing tick wide; curves refitted.

## Scrumper (left out)

`snipRev 11` (par time per level). On #175's bots the more forgiving cut timing (wider chutes, larger leaves) lifted
every level: good 132.5 against 97 before (+37 %), with great, near-perfect and elite bunched together (162.5,
164.5, 173), so an ordinary player's score would jump. Joe asked for it to be reverted to the stricter version and
re-measured; see `claude/x200-snip-parked` and the PR description for the latest numbers.
