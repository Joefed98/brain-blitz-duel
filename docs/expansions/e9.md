# Expansion E9: Rally, Fission and Flightpath to 200

26 Oct 2026. Gameplay change, one PR. It rebuilds the parked expansions of Rally (#154), Fission (#155) and
Flightpath (branch `claude/flightpath-e5-parked`, from #162) to the arcade rule agreed with Joe on 26 Oct, and
raises all three to 200 (`ROUND_MAX`, docs/scoring-200.md). New rounds deal the new stamps; every older stamp
replays and scores exactly as before (expansion-e9-check: 92 runs recorded on unmodified main on every older
revision, unstamped included).

| Game | New stamp | Before (strictRev) | World |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rally | `rallyRev 10` | `rallyRev 8` | 420 × 784 (was 390 × 730) |
| Fission | `burstRev 8` | `burstRev 6` | 390 × 650, higher arena (ceiling from 24 px, higher bounces) |
| Flightpath | `bullRev 8` | `bullRev 6` | 390 × 728 (boards at five distances) |

The stamps are one above the highest in #154 / #155 / the parked branch (rallyRev 9, burstRev 7, bullRev 7 were
never live and are not dealt or defined here).

## The arcade rule

Every player in a room plays the identical run, so fairness within a round comes from the room seed.

1. **Guaranteed opportunities.** Every feature is placed and timed at init from the room seed (a stream of
   the game's own), the same for every player and never from how a run has gone; every run holds the same
   number of chances (Rally's 6 gold targets, Fission's 6 golden atoms and 7 power-ups, Flightpath's
   dead-centre on 6 to 9 darts, worth the same 42 at most on every seed). expansion-e9-check proves that two (three) different players on one seed
   — a weak bot, an elite bot and random input — see the same schedule all run long, that every pad move and
   every falling power-up is the scheduled one, and Flightpath's ceiling on all 260 tuning and fresh seeds.
2. **Skill headroom.** Features pay a modest fixed amount on top of the base, and pay more the better the
   play (the luck-check tables below, paired on the same seeds).

## Rally, `rallyRev 10` (`src/lib/arcade/rally.ts`, `rally.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/rally.tsx`)

- **Kept:** #154's taller court (420 × 784; both bumpers on it); spin shots (the paddle's movement at contact
  curves the return; a way to aim, it pays nothing); the two pink pads exactly as today — each in its own half,
  a gold ball striking one scores it (6 units) and the pad jumps to its half's next spot. The spots are a list
  of 48 drawn from the seed at init (they always were, on every revision; rallyRev 10 draws them on its own
  stream), so every player gets the same spots in the same order; the check proves every move lands on spot
  k + 2(j + 1) of that list.
- **Removed:** #154's gliding pads, the ×2 multi-ball pad and twin, and the milestone bonus.
- **Gold bonus target (new):** 6 a round (`RALLY_V10_BONUS_COUNT`), one in each 9 s window from 4 s at a seeded
  0–3 s into it, at a seeded place in the upper half of the court at least 46 px from either bumper's centre.
  12 px radius (24 across; about 24 phone px in the 414-wide box), up for 4 s (`RALLY_V10_BONUS_LIFE`, about
  two returns) with a countdown ring that reddens in its last second. Any ball (blue or gold) touching it
  scores **+6** (`RALLY_V10_BONUS_PTS`); it does not deflect the ball; a miss costs nothing. HUD: "TARGET n/6"
  top right, with "GOLD n · PADS n" under it. A struck target bursts gold with "+6" rising (still under reduced
  motion), the court bulbs flash, and the shared layer's "GOLD TARGET +6" moment (replay moment `gold-target`).
- **Units:** 5 a gold-ball hit, 6 a pink pad and **1 a blue return** (new: a run that loses the blue ball before
  the first gold ball still scores a little) on `RALLY_V10_BAND_CURVE` (190 at 270 units, flat from 180 to 240:
  see "Against the gate"), plus the targets, at most 200.

## Fission, `burstRev 8` (`src/lib/arcade/burst.ts`, `burst.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/burst.tsx`)

- **Kept:** #155's higher arena; the wave-clear time bonus (3 within 5 s of a wave appearing, 2 within 8 s, 1
  within 12 s; up to 12); the DOUBLE beam (8 s) and FREEZE (3 s).
- **Power-ups on a fixed schedule:** 7 a run (`BURST_V8_DROPS`), one in each 12 s window from 6 s at a seeded
  0–2 s into it, DOUBLE and FREEZE alternating from a seeded first, falling from the top at a seeded x. Atoms
  drop nothing on this revision (the old random BURST and SHIELD drops are gone). Power-ups pay nothing.
  The DOUBLE beam's two beams now sit 7 px either side of the ship (#155 had 14): under the smallest atom's
  and the golden atom's radius, so the pair can never straddle a target centred over the ship.
- **Golden atom:** 6 a run, each in a 13 s window from 8 s at a seeded 0–3 s, at a seeded spot in a band below
  the lowest the ceiling ever comes, up for 4 s with a countdown ring of gold pixels (red in its last second).
  A beam that reaches it takes it for **+6** (up to 36).
- **Removed:** chain reactions (shockwave rings).
- **Clean wave (new):** a wave cleared with no missed shot adds **+5** (up to 25), on top of the time bonus. A
  shot is one tap; a double shot's two beams are one shot, which hits if either beam does. A shot that ends
  with every beam at the ceiling having hit nothing (no atom, no golden atom) is a miss, and one miss ends the
  bonus for that wave. A shot counts for the wave the counter shows (the next one between waves). HUD:
  "CLEAN hit/fired" top right (green, red once the wave has a miss), "MISS" and "CLEAN +5" pops.
- **Units:** atoms hit less 5 a life lost, but **never below a quarter of the atoms hit** (new, so a run that
  hits atoms always scores), on `BURST_V8_BAND_CURVE` (128 at 100 units, the most any bot reaches, then on to 136
  at 140, which only perfect play reaches); lasting the 90 s adds 10; then the features.

## Flightpath, `bullRev 8` (`src/lib/arcade/bullseye.ts`, `bullseye.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/bullseye.tsx`)

- **Kept:** everything from #162's Flightpath expansion: the taller range with each dart's board at one of five
  distances (seeded per dart), the drifting (rail) board, the balloons, the wind gusts and the bull streak (the
  n-th bull in a row adds 2 × (n − 2) from the third; now counted up to 30, was 50).
- **Dead-centre ring (new):** a white hairline ring inside the bull at **a quarter of its radius**
  (`BULLSEYE_V8_DEAD_DIV`, drawn exactly at its scoring edge, shown whenever the 10 is the centre ring). A bull
  inside it adds **+7** on top (up to 42), and keeps the streak going like any bull. HUD: "Dart n/10 · streak
  s · dead-centre d" (gold while a streak runs); a dead-centre dart pops "DEAD CENTRE +7". The brief asked for
  about half the bull's radius and a small extra; with the band tool's fixed bots a half-radius ring paid
  near-perfect (five of its 10-centred bulls inside) almost as much as elite (six), so a quarter it is (two
  against three and a half). A seed has six to nine darts with the 10 in the centre, so on a six-dart seed a
  perfect run needs all six dead-centre darts for 200: 7 a dart, up to 42, is what keeps the ceiling at 200 on
  every seed with near-perfect's median under 175.
- **Score:** the dart total on `BULLSEYE_V8_BAND_CURVE` (142 at 118, an inner bull every dart; on to 150 at 130, which takes balloons too), plus streak and dead-centre points.

## Tuning

**With the skill-band tool's fixed bots** (Session A, #175, `docs/arcade-bands-elite.md`): competent bots
(`BotCtx.fixes`) and seven levels, weak to elite plus flawless (no human error at all, used for each seed's
ceiling). `bun scripts/expansion-e9-bands.ts --set=both --random --off` plays every level on the **same** room seeds
on the old revision and the new one (paired), through the server's encode + replay path, with the fixed bots on
both, so "before" is like for like. The E9 bots' feature play sits on top of those fixes: Rally's go for a target
when it is safe and keep both balls when the finger can (the fixed bots' `canBoth`); Fission's walk under a golden
atom and fire when the beam's line is clear, and don't fire again at an atom their own beam has just split;
Flightpath's are the fixed bot (sway on drifting darts, the balloon policy) on the parked range, unchanged for the
dead-centre ring (a player aims at the middle of the bull anyway). Old revisions draw exactly the random numbers
they always drew, so every older check's fixed-seed proof is unchanged.

Seeds: **tuning** = room seeds 0–59 (the curves were fitted on these only), **fresh** = 10000–10199, the band tool's
fresh set, never used for tuning by anyone (measured once, on the final constants). The curves were fitted by a small
search over each curve's knots (and, for Flightpath, the ring and its points) against the gate on the tuning seeds,
with the per-seed ceiling as a hard constraint and a cap on how steeply a curve rises; every number below is a real
replayed run on the final constants. "Features ignored" is the new revision with the bots not playing the features.

## Against the gate

- **Good within about ±10 % of before:** Rally +2 % / +11 %, Fission −10 % / −6 %, Flightpath +1 % / +3 %. Fission's
  "before" is 150 from good up (its burstRev 6 curve tops out for any competent player, as the band report found),
  so like for like puts its good level at 135–141.
- **Near-perfect median 165–175, top 10 % at most about 185:** medians all in range but Rally's fresh 164. Top 10 %
  186–190 in four of six cells (Rally's fresh 188, Fission's 186, Flightpath's 190 / 186): **not forced**, see below.
- **Elite median at least about 175, top 10 % 190–200:** Fission 177 / 179, Flightpath 180 / 183; Rally 174 / 173,
  a point or two under. Every elite top 10 % is 190–200.
- **Weak and average never 0; random near 0:** no weak or average run scored 0 on any seed. Random play's median is
  0 for Rally and Flightpath and 7–10 for Fission (random shots hit atoms; best 38–39).
- **Skill check:** every feature's median payout rises or holds from weak to elite on the same seeds; flawless play
  collects the most. Rally's gold target is 18 from great to elite on the tuning seeds (its means rise steadily);
  Fission's clean wave holds at 15 from great up and its fast wave at 2 from good up; Flightpath's streak is full (30)
  from near-perfect up, so above near-perfect the dead-centre ring (14 → 21 → 42) and the dart total separate players.

**Why the near-perfect top 10 % isn't forced lower.**

- *Rally* runs are noisy within a seed: on the same seed elite beats near-perfect by 14 units at the median, but by
  anywhere from −45 to +82 (p10–p90), because a gold ball kept or lost early cascades. A near-perfect run's
  units go from 114 to 226 (p10–p90), past elite's median (181), so a curve that holds near-perfect's top 10 % at
  185 caps elite with it. The fit already flattens the curve from 180 to 240 units (158 → 163) for this.
- *Fission* near-perfect and elite hit 85 and 87 atom units at the median (the 90 s and the waves' size cap how many
  atoms anyone hits); the gap is in the golden atoms (24 against 30) and lives.
- *Flightpath* near-perfect fills the streak (30) and lands two dead-centre darts in ten (14); its lucky runs land
  three or four. A smaller ring or less per dart would drop the ceiling under 200 on six-dart seeds.

## Ceilings (perfect play)

`bun scripts/expansion-e9-ceiling.ts` plays each game's oracle (`scripts/expansion-e9-oracles.ts`) and the band
tool's flawless level (two tries) through the server's encode + replay path on all 260 tuning and fresh seeds, and
keeps the best replayed score, a lower bound on that seed's ceiling. **All three reach 200 on all 260 seeds.**

| Game | Seeds at 200 | How |
|---|---|---|
| Flightpath | 260 / 260 | Exact launch inversion. expansion-e9-check also proves it dart by dart on every seed: inner bull every dart, dead centre on every 10-centred dart (six to nine), streak 30. |
| Fission | 260 / 260 | The ship snaps (as a touch in the control strip does) and every shot is checked on a fork of the sim first: 197–225 atom units against the 100 the band tools' best reach, every golden atom, every wave clean. |
| Rally | 260 / 260 | The paddle goes where the finger is put and every return is chosen on forks of the sim: 343+ units against the 270 the curve tops out at (190), and at least three gold targets. |

## Assumptions

- **Session A's tool is #175, not yet merged** when this was tuned. This branch merges its branch for the bots
  (adopting its `human.ts` and its fixes to the Rally, Fission and Flightpath bots, with the E9 feature play on top);
  it is pushed once #175 is on main, by merging main.
- **Fresh seeds** are the band tool's 10000–10199, measured once on the final constants. The earlier fresh set
  (60–299) is retired: it had been looked at more than once before the fixed bots.
- **Flightpath's ring and points moved from the brief** (half the bull's radius, a small extra) to a quarter of
  it at 7 a dart, up to 42 (above). The streak cap stays 30.
- **Rally's pink pads** keep today's "+3" label in the court (as main draws it); in units a pad is worth 6, as on
  every revision.
- **Fission's DOUBLE beam's** two beams sit 7 px either side of the ship (#155 had 14), so the pair can never
  straddle a small atom or the golden atom.
- The canvas mockups' look was followed from the existing renderers' style (the game frame, glow, countdown rings);
  Joe judges the final look on the phone.
