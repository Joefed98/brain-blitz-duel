# Joulz card art and frames

26 card arts (8 free, 7 tier, 10 shop on the five rarities, 1 Season 1 pass) and 8 frames, as agreed on the canvas "Card art and frames".

- Rank pill: tier and division in the tier colour, a divider, then the rating in bold white with tabular numbers, on a dark translucent pill, so it reads on every art.
- Legendary shop arts (Aurora, Diamond Plate) animate with a slow shimmer (a light sweep about every 6 s); it stops under Reduce Motion and in Battery saver.
- `svg/` has every art at 2x; `cards.json` lists them with where they come from, and the frame styles; `source/cards.py` draws them.
