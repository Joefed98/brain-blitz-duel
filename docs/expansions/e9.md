# Expansion E9: Rally, Fission and Flightpath to 200

26 Oct 2026. Gameplay change, one PR. It rebuilds the parked expansions of Rally (#154), Fission (#155) and
Flightpath (branch `claude/flightpath-e5-parked`, from #162) to the arcade rule agreed with Joe on 26 Oct, and
raises all three to 200 (`ROUND_MAX`, docs/scoring-200.md). New rounds deal the new stamps; every older stamp
replays and scores exactly as before (expansion-e9-check: 92 runs recorded on unmodified main on every older
revision, unstamped included).

| Game | New stamp | Before (strictRev) | World |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rally | `rallyRev 10` | `rallyRev 8` | 420 × 784 (was 390 × 730) |
| Fission | `burstRev 8` | `burstRev 6` | 390 × 650, higher arena (ceiling from 24 px, higher bounces) |
| Flightpath | `bullRev 8` | `bullRev 6` | 390 × 728 (boards at five distances) |

The stamps are one above the highest in #154 / #155 / the parked branch (rallyRev 9, burstRev 7, bullRev 7 were
never live and are not dealt or defined here).

## The arcade rule

Every player in a room plays the identical run, so fairness within a round comes from the room seed.

1. **Guaranteed opportunities.** Every feature is placed and timed at init from the room seed (a stream of
   the game's own), the same for every player and never from how a run has gone; every run holds the same
   number of chances (Rally's 6 gold targets, Fission's 6 golden atoms and 7 power-ups, Flightpath's
   dead-centre on at least 6 darts). expansion-e9-check proves that two (three) different players on one seed
   — a weak bot, an elite bot and random input — see the same schedule all run long, that every pad move and
   every falling power-up is the scheduled one, and Flightpath's ceiling on all 300 seeds.
2. **Skill headroom.** Features pay a modest fixed amount on top of the base, and pay more the better the
   play (the luck-check tables below, paired on the same seeds).

## Rally, `rallyRev 10` (`src/lib/arcade/rally.ts`, `rally.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/rally.tsx`)

- **Kept:** #154's taller court (420 × 784; both bumpers on it); spin shots (the paddle's movement at contact
  curves the return; a way to aim, it pays nothing); the two pink pads exactly as today — each in its own half,
  a gold ball striking one scores it (6 units) and the pad jumps to its half's next spot. The spots are a list
  of 48 drawn from the seed at init (they always were, on every revision; rallyRev 10 draws them on its own
  stream), so every player gets the same spots in the same order; the check proves every move lands on spot
  k + 2j of that list.
- **Removed:** #154's gliding pads, the ×2 multi-ball pad and twin, and the milestone bonus.
- **Gold bonus target (new):** 6 a round (`RALLY_V10_BONUS_COUNT`), one in each 9 s window from 4 s at a seeded
  0–3 s into it, at a seeded place in the upper half of the court at least 46 px from either bumper's centre.
  12 px radius (24 across; about 24 phone px in the 414-wide box), up for 4 s (`RALLY_V10_BONUS_LIFE`, about
  two returns) with a countdown ring that reddens in its last second. Any ball (blue or gold) touching it
  scores **+6** (`RALLY_V10_BONUS_PTS`); it does not deflect the ball; a miss costs nothing. HUD: "TARGET n/6"
  top right, with "GOLD n · PADS n" under it. A struck target bursts gold with "+6" rising (still under reduced
  motion), the court bulbs flash, and the shared layer's "GOLD TARGET +6" moment (replay moment `gold-target`).
- **Units:** 5 a gold-ball hit, 6 a pink pad and **1 a blue return** (new: a run that loses the blue ball before
  the first gold ball still scores a little) on `RALLY_V10_BAND_CURVE` (to 178), plus the targets, to 200.

## Fission, `burstRev 8` (`src/lib/arcade/burst.ts`, `burst.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/burst.tsx`)

- **Kept:** #155's higher arena; the wave-clear time bonus (3 within 5 s of a wave appearing, 2 within 8 s, 1
  within 12 s; up to 12); the DOUBLE beam (8 s) and FREEZE (3 s).
- **Power-ups on a fixed schedule:** 7 a run (`BURST_V8_DROPS`), one in each 12 s window from 6 s at a seeded
  0–2 s into it, DOUBLE and FREEZE alternating from a seeded first, falling from the top at a seeded x. Atoms
  drop nothing on this revision (the old random BURST and SHIELD drops are gone). Power-ups pay nothing.
  The DOUBLE beam's two beams now sit 7 px either side of the ship (#155 had 14): under the smallest atom's
  and the golden atom's radius, so the pair can never straddle a target centred over the ship.
- **Golden atom:** 6 a run, each in a 13 s window from 8 s at a seeded 0–3 s, at a seeded spot in a band below
  the lowest the ceiling ever comes, up for 4 s with a countdown ring of gold pixels (red in its last second).
  A beam that reaches it takes it for **+6** (up to 36).
- **Removed:** chain reactions (shockwave rings).
- **Clean wave (new):** a wave cleared with no missed shot adds **+5** (up to 25), on top of the time bonus. A
  shot is one tap; a double shot's two beams are one shot, which hits if either beam does. A shot that ends
  with every beam at the ceiling having hit nothing (no atom, no golden atom) is a miss, and one miss ends the
  bonus for that wave. A shot counts for the wave the counter shows (the next one between waves). HUD:
  "CLEAN hit/fired" top right (green, red once the wave has a miss), "MISS" and "CLEAN +5" pops.
- **Units:** atoms hit less 5 a life lost, but **never below a quarter of the atoms hit** (new, so a run that
  hits atoms always scores), on `BURST_V8_BAND_CURVE` (to 136); lasting the 90 s adds 10; then the features.

## Flightpath, `bullRev 8` (`src/lib/arcade/bullseye.ts`, `bullseye.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/bullseye.tsx`)

- **Kept:** everything from #162's Flightpath expansion: the taller range with each dart's board at one of five
  distances (seeded per dart), the drifting (rail) board, the balloons, the wind gusts and the bull streak (the
  n-th bull in a row adds 2 × (n − 2) from the third; now counted up to 30, was 50).
- **Dead-centre ring (new):** a white hairline ring inside the bull at half its radius
  (`BULLSEYE_V8_DEAD_DIV`, drawn exactly at its scoring edge, shown whenever the 10 is the centre ring). A bull
  inside it adds **+4** on top (up to 24), and keeps the streak going like any bull. HUD: "Dart n/10 · streak
  s · dead-centre d" (gold while a streak runs); a dead-centre dart pops "DEAD CENTRE +4".
- **Score:** the dart total on `BULLSEYE_V8_BAND_CURVE` (150 at 118), plus streak and dead-centre points.

## Tuning

`bun scripts/expansion-e9-bands.ts --set=both --random --off` plays every level on the **same** room seeds on the
old revision and the new one (paired), through the server's encode + replay path. The skill-band tool gained two
levels for this, `weak` (reaction 380 ms, aim sd 13 px, slips 18 %) and `elite` (reaction 145 ms, timing sd 8 ms,
aim sd 1.6 px); see "Assumptions". The bots learnt the features: Rally's go for a target when it is safe
(another ball isn't landing straight after, the contact point needed is well inside the paddle), and better
players take a gold ball even when the blue one lands soon after, when the finger can get across in time;
Fission's walk under a golden atom and fire when the beam's line is clear and the atom will still be there,
and no longer fire again at an atom their own beam has just split (a perception bug that made every level
miss about as often); Flightpath's never changed (its features are where a dart lands). Old revisions draw
exactly the random numbers they always drew, so every older check's fixed-seed proof is unchanged.

The curves were fitted on the tuning seeds only (`RALLY_V10_BAND_CURVE`, `BURST_V8_BAND_CURVE`,
`BULLSEYE_V8_BAND_CURVE`), with a cap on how steeply they rise so a lucky run can't jump far; the fresh seeds
were then measured once, then once more after the Rally bots' fix and Fission's refit (so they were seen twice).
"Features ignored" is the new revision with the bots not playing the features (Rally's targets, Fission's golden
atoms and DOUBLE icons; Flightpath has nothing to switch off).

  - Flightpath: 166 / 186 on both sets.

- **Skill check:** every feature's median payout rises, or holds, from weak to elite on the same seeds. The flat
  stretches:
  - Rally's target sits at 12 for average and good and at 18 from great up (its means rise steadily, 7 → 20).
  - Fission's clean wave is 15 from great up, and its fast wave 2 from good up (the bots clear about five waves,
    most in 10–20 s).
  - Good and near-perfect are more than 15 points apart in every game, so there is no case to report there.

## Ceilings (perfect play)

`bun scripts/expansion-e9-ceiling.ts` plays an oracle for each game (`scripts/expansion-e9-oracles.ts`) through
the server's encode + replay path on all 300 seeds (tuning and fresh). Each replayed score is a lower bound on
that seed's ceiling.

| Game | Seeds at 200 | Lowest | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flightpath | 300 / 300 | 200 | Exact launch inversion. expansion-e9-check also proves analytically, on all 300 seeds, that each dart can land dead centre. |
| Fission | 300 / 300 | 200 | The ship snaps (as a touch in the control strip does) and every shot is checked on a fork of the sim first. The oracle lands 197–225 atom units against the 100 the curve tops out at, with every golden atom and every wave clean. |
| Rally | 296 / 300 | 196 | Seeds 86, 89, 266 and 294 reach 196: the full base (178) and 3 of the 6 targets. The missed ones come in the opening seconds with only the blue ball in play, and the oracle plans one contact ahead. Two-contact aiming should reach them, but that is unproven. 196 is within the "few points" the brief allows. |

## Assumptions

- **Session A's elite level was not on main** (no open or merged PR "arcade skill-band tool: elite level" on 26
  Oct). The skill-band tool gained its own `weak` and `elite` levels here (`EXTRA_LEVELS` in
  `scripts/arcade-skill-bands/human.ts`, opt-in, so the four routine levels and every older check are
  unchanged). When Session A lands, merge it and re-run `bun scripts/expansion-e9-bands.ts` with its elite
  level. The curves may want a touch.
- The fresh seeds were measured more than once: after the Rally bots' fix, and after Fission's refit, which
  made the clean wave worth 5 (from 4). They were never used to fit a knot, but they are no longer unseen.
- Rally's pink pads keep today's "+3" label in the court (as main draws it). In units a pad is worth 6, as on
  every revision.
- Fission's DOUBLE beam's two beams sit 7 px either side of the ship (#155 had 14), so the pair can never
  straddle a small atom or the golden atom.
