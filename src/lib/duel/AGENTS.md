- Word Search draws from a script-built, server-only bank (scripts/word-search-bank.ts); why: each word's placement is checked offline, not at room start.
- Word Search category lists live in src/lib/duel/wordSearchWords.ts and must be followed by re-running the bank script; why: the bank is derived from them.
- Number Pyramid draws four rising-difficulty boards from a script-built, server-only bank (scripts/number-pyramid-bank.ts); answers are (cell, tray index) pairs graded against the stored tray and solution; why: uniqueness is proven offline and tile values never come from the device.
- Dig is a shared integer water sim in src/lib/duel/dig.ts replayed on the server from the (step, cell) dig log, over boards from the script-built, server-only bank (scripts/dig-bank.ts); why: phone, grader and generator must flow water identically, and the intended route never reaches a device.
- Letter Wheel draws from a script-built, server-only bank (scripts/letter-wheel-bank.ts, ranked by scripts/data/word-frequency.txt); every board has exactly 8 or 9 crossword words, and submitted words are matched server-side against those slots while other dictionary words are neutral; why: layouts are proven offline and answers never reach a device.
- Trail draws from a script-built, server-only bank (scripts/trail-bank.ts); the client sends only the cell path, graded against stored givens and solution; why: uniqueness is proven offline by the shared backtracking solver in trail.ts.
- Intruder Detection runs a fixed clock-driven timeline per board (card 1 s, show 5 s, blank 1 s, pick 3 s, hold 1 s) derived from the round start, and shuffled boards store `perm` (after[i] comes from cells[perm[i]]) with answers indexed in the shown grid; why: identical timing for all players and the grid never moves.
