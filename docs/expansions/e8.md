## E8 · Wavefront wavRev 9 (world expansion and depth, 25 Oct 2026)

Wavefront is raised to 200 (`ROUND_MAX.wavefront`). Rooms and practice deal `wavRev 9`
(`WAV_REV_EXPAND`, `src/lib/arcade/wavefront.ts`, "World expansion"); every older revision plays and
scores exactly as before (its step function is untouched; the new rules live in their own `stepX`,
and every shared helper that learned about wavRev 9 is gated on the state's `x9` marker).

### More world

- **Taller arena**: 390 × 800 (`WAV_X_H`; wavRev 6–8 were 390 × 730). The extra 70 px sits between the
  formation and the ship (the ship stays 66 px above the bottom; drags start in the bottom quarter).
  At 414 × 896 (iPhone 11 home-screen app) the world draws at 0.965× (377 px wide, the screen fill's
  sides either side; Wavefront is in `ARENA_FILL_SIDES`).
- **Longer waves**: 36 ships a wave (30 before). Wave 1 is a 6 × 6 grid (one more row from the same seeded
  stream); waves 2–5 keep their Tuning A shapes and armour cores and add a row of six escorts under the
  shape (`WAV_FORMS_X`).
- **A gunship** (mid-boss, 96 px, 16 hit points, crimson) flies in after wave 3 and counts as a wave; the
  mothership (36 hit points) still follows wave 5 and its kill still ends the run with the timed bonus.
- **Retune** for the bigger load and the longer drop: the gun fires every 16 ticks (20), bolts fly 11 px a
  tick (10), still three centre bolts in the air; red bolts start at 5 px a tick (4) and every 75 ticks (90).
  Same 90 s, the same divers, saucer, march and drops (waves 3 and 4 still drop three quarters of a row).

### Skill features

- **Power-ups** (the existing laser drop, extended): a shot-down saucer, armoured core or boss cannon drops
  one of three seeded kinds (`WAV_DROP_*`, one falling at a time): the laser (5 piercing shots, as
  before), the **spread shot** (8 volleys with two extra bolts angled out at 2 px a tick) and the **shield**
  (absorbs the next red bolt or ram for up to 12 s; a cyan bubble round the ship).
- **Hit streak**: every hit in a row (ships, armour, divers, saucers, boss parts) grows the streak; its
  multiplier is ×2 from 8, ×3 from 16, ×4 from 24 (`wavStreakMult`) and each hit adds the multiplier less
  one to the streak points. A centre bolt that flies off without hitting, or a lost life, ends it; spread
  bolts, volleys fired during a fly-in and a shielded hit never do. The HUD's second line shows
  "STREAK 17 ×3" (gold from ×2) and the power-up held. Hits keep showing the sparkle, never points.
- **Boss weak points** (gunship and mothership): two cannons (4 hits each, 16 px columns at the guns; a
  destroyed one stops firing, smokes and drops a power-up) and a core iris that opens for half of every
  150 ticks from the boss's arrival: a hit on the open core takes 3 hit points.
- The bonus UFO (saucer) is unchanged apart from its drop's kind.

### Scoring (wavRev 9)

- **Base** (today's achievement): ships + armour hits + saucers + 15 a wave (the gunship too) + every boss
  hit point taken, on `WAV_V9_BAND_CURVE` to 140 at a perfect run (`WAV_PAR_X` = 330), plus the kill's
  speed bonus to 10 (as wavRev 5–8).
- **Edge** (up to 50, only from the features): streak points + 4 an open-core hit + 6 a cannon destroyed +
  3 a power-up caught + 4 a shield save (`wavEdgeUnits`), on `WAV_X_EDGE_CURVE`, steep only at the top
  (110 units → 2, 160 → 12, 220 → 30, 300 → 50).
- Total: `bandTotal(parts, acted, 200)`; the reveal's band lines show the base, then "Boss destroyed (+k) ·
  streaks, weak points and power-ups +n".
- Fitted by quantile-matching the wavRev 9 base units to wavRev 8's points over the same bots and seeds
  (`scripts/wavefront-expand-bands.ts`), then the edge curve by level.

## E8 expansion: Scrumper (snip), snipRev 10

25 Oct 2026. Scrumper's world expansion and skill features (docs/scoring-200.md). Older rounds (snipRev 1–9)
play and score exactly as before; rooms deal snipRev 10.

### The world

- **A tall course.** Each level is a course 360 px wide and 1,200 px tall (`SnipCourse.h`), about 1.8 screens,
  seen through a 360 × 672 view (`SNIP_X_VIEW_H`; it fills a 414 × 772 arena box at the largest uniform scale,
  so the iPhone 11 home-screen app shows it edge to edge). There is no horizontal camera any more (the course is
  the view's width, solid walls at both sides); the vertical camera rides in the sim state (`camY`, whole px, eased
  a sixth of the way each tick), so the phone, the server's reveal, the effects layer and the cut mapping all see
  the same view. The renderer draws the backdrop with a slow parallax, apple trees up both walls, the course, then
  the view overlays.
- **The opening pan.** Each level opens with a 1 s pan (`SNIP_X_PAN_TICKS` = 60) from the basket up to the fruit
  (a smoothstep in the sim). The sim holds still and ignores cuts during it; the level clock runs (14 s a level).
- **Six levels a run** (`SNIP_X_LEVELS`; 2 easy, 2 medium, 2 hard from the bank, on their own seed stream) instead
  of ten, since each course is longer. The run stays 60 s.
- **Course pieces.** Pegs are the picker's crooks (extended to every fruit: a free fruit falling past a hook from
  above is snagged on a vine); **rings** are new: a brass hoop that snags a free fruit passing through its middle
  (within `SNIP_X_RING_REACH` = 14 px) from any direction, then holds it on a vine. Slopes are planks and gutters,
  bouncers springy branches, as before; most courses end in a wooden chute over the basket (two boards sloping in,
  two upright). Wasp nests sit beside the paths. Falling fruit is capped at 12 px a tick (`SNIP_X_MAX_SPEED`, air
  drag) so nothing tunnels through a 9 px plank.

### The features

- **Several fruits a level** (1–3), each on its own ropes; fruits never touch each other. Every fruit can be fed.
- **Golden apple**: on levels 2, 4 and 6 one fruit (a seeded pick among the course's `golds`) is golden for the
  level's first 7 s (`SNIP_X_GOLD_TICKS`, the pan included; drawn gold with a soft glow and a gold ring that runs
  down). Fed in time it counts; after that it ripens into an ordinary apple (`gold` 2).
- **Wind**: each level deals one of its course's three presets (amplitude 0–11/256 px a tick², period 160–300
  ticks): a signed integer triangle wave that pushes the ropes and the fruit they hold, so they swing. A falling
  fruit is not blown (it keeps courses predictable once released). Shown by a windsock at the top left and
  drifting streaks and leaves (no streaks with reduced effects; still with reduced motion).
- **Fewest cuts**: a level counts strokes (swipe inputs on consecutive ticks are one stroke; a missed swipe counts
  too), shown as "n/par cuts" in the HUD line. Every fruit fed in at most the course's `par` strokes is a par level.
  Par is the most strokes any deal's perfect stored plan needs; on some courses one swipe across two neighbouring
  ropes beats the naive count.
- **Stars along the way**: two golden leaves a course; the first counts in the base units (2), the second is a
  bonus (2).
- **The level map** at the top right: the whole course, the basket, fruit, pegs, rings, nests and leaves, with the
  current view boxed (view space, display only).

### Deals and proofs

A level's deal comes from the run's seed: a level-wide pin shift (−10, 0, +10 px), one of three wind presets and
(on gold levels) the golden fruit, so every course has 9 physical deals. Every course's every deal has stored plans
in `scripts/fixtures/snip-expand-proofs.json`: `win` (the fastest found that feeds every fruit) and, for each fruit
that may be golden, `best[g]` (a perfect level: every fruit, par, both leaves, that fruit fed while golden). Plans are human-paced: the first cut no sooner than 24 ticks after the pan,
strokes at least 12 ticks apart, a stroke's consecutive cuts within 70 px of each other, every planned cut finding
its rope alive (a vine only after its catch). The courses were laid by `scripts/snip-expand-design.ts`, which traces
each fruit's fall with a reference plan and lays the pieces along it, then keeps a candidate only when every deal
has a win plan and a perfect plan for at least one golden fruit, and its forgiveness (the share of ±24-tick shifts
of the reference plan that still feed every fruit) fits its band (easy ≥ 0.45, medium 0.20–0.62, hard ≤ 0.40).
The bank is **36 courses** (11 easy, 12 medium, 13 hard; hard ones took the longest to find:
the steering of a fast fall into the basket fails often, so every multi-fruit course has a narrow chute over the
basket: ±80 px easy, ±66 medium, ±56 hard).

### Scoring (snipRev 10)

- Units: each level's fruits share 18 (`18 × fed ÷ fruits`) and a leaf adds 2, so 120 is every fruit and a leaf on
  all six levels. `SNIP_V10_BAND_CURVE` turns units into points up to 140; feeding every fruit of every level adds
  up to 10 for speed (the full 10 by 48 s, falling to 0 at the buzzer; `SNIP_X_FINISH_MIN` = 36 s).
- Feature bonus (`snipFeatureBonus`, at most 50): raw points 5 per golden apple fed in time (3 a run), 2.5 per par
  level (6), 2 per second leaf (6) and 8 for a perfect run (every level at par with both leaves and all three
  golden apples), 50 at the top; the bonus is 50 × (raw ÷ 50)², in integer half points (raw2² ÷ 200), so a little
  use of the features adds little (average players about 5, near-perfect ones about 15) and only strong use of all of
  them nears 50.
- Total: `bandTotal(parts, acted, 200)`; no input scores 0. Grading's "correct" flag is 200 on snipRev 10 (150
  before).

## E8: Tilt expansion (tiltRev 7, 25 Oct 2026)

Part of expansion session E8 (Wavefront, Scrumper, Tilt). Tilt's round maximum is 200 (`ROUND_MAX`); new
rooms deal `tiltRev: 7` (`TILT_REV_EXPAND`). Every older revision plays and scores exactly as before.

### What changed

**More world.** Three bigger mazes (`TILT_X_SIZES` 13, 15 and 17 cells across at 30 px a cell, against
tiltRev 6's four mazes of 11–13 cells in a 360 px disc) in a taller world, 440 × 820 (`TILT_X_W` ×
`TILT_X_H`, was 440 × 470), which fills the iPhone 11 home-screen app's arena box (414 × 772) edge to edge.
The second and third mazes are wider than the screen: the renderer's camera follows the marbles still
rolling (their middle, pulled a third of the way back toward the disc's centre so more maze is in view),
eased, and the drawing turns about the screen's centre, so the marbles stay near the middle while the board
spins round them. Display only: the sim turns gravity into the maze frame by the angle alone, whichever
point the drawing turns about, and the drag input (horizontal deltas) is unchanged. A marble the camera
cannot show gets an arrow on the screen's edge in its colour. The cup sits at the rim cell whose route is
nearest `TILT_X_ROUTE` (50, 58, 52 cells for the blue marble; the third maze also has the amber one), so the
mazes have far more off the route to find. Black holes 1, 2, 3; the amber marble from maze 3
(`TILT_X_TWO_FROM`), as tiltRev 6's last half; no red chaser.

**Skill features** (all seeded on the decoration stream, gated on `xp: 1` in the state):
- *Ice zones*: frosted cells (`maze.ice`): the marble keeps its speed there (no rolling drag) up to 4.8 px a
  tick (`TILT_X_ICE_CAP`, 3.6 elsewhere), so it slides on past a turn or into a hole. Two or three patches of
  five cells on the routes, and every quick star's side passage.
- *Keys and gates*: the old padlock gate generalised. A gate across the blue route (between 30 % and 75 % of
  the way) and, in the two-marble maze, one across the amber route's own part; each is a wall for both marbles
  until either marble rolls over its key (brass, then verdigris; key and gate share a colour), which waits two
  to ten cells into a side passage on the start's side. `tiltCupReachableX` proves every key reachable with
  every gate shut (gates opening as their keys are taken) and both cups reachable after; the dealer re-deals
  any seed that fails it or has a maze with no key (`tiltSeedReachableX`, `tiltExpandHasKeys`; about 5 % of
  seeds).
- *Time-bonus stars* ("quick stars"): two per maze, three to seven cells into an icy side passage off the
  route's first half, each with a ring that drains over 20 s from the maze's start (`TILT_X_QUICK_WINDOW`).
  Taken within 12 s (`TILT_X_QUICK_FULL`, the ring still pale gold) one is worth 8, then less as the ring
  turns amber and runs out (`tiltQuickPoints`); after 20 s it is gone. All six add 2 more (`TILT_X_SWEEP`).
- Kept: two marbles at once (blue and amber, each to its own cup), the gold stars at the black holes'
  mouths (two per maze), the black holes.

**Score** (`tiltXBandParts`, `scoreX`): mazes 40, progress through the maze being played up to 39, stars 5
on `TILT_V7_BAND_CURVE` (units 0 45 85 115 130 150 → 0 45 60 72 104 140), finishing all three adds up to
10 for speed (full by 72.5 s, falling to 0 at the buzzer; `TILT_X_FINISH_MIN` 55 s), quick stars add up to
50: `bandTotal(parts, acted, 200)`. Not routed through `bandCurveOf` / `bandStrict` (no strict row). A run
with no input scores 0. The extra 50 above 150 can only come from quick stars.

**Texts.** Intro: GOAL unchanged; HOW "Drag left or right to spin the maze." / "Keys open gates; grab quick
stars early." / "From maze 3, two marbles, each to its own cup." (arcade-batch4-check allows three lines of
46 characters); SCORE "Mazes, progress and stars; early quick stars add more. All 3 mazes quickly: up to
{max}." The "?" help adds keys and gates, quick stars and ice, and its score line comes from the new curve
(`liveBandHelp`, ending "Up to 200."). The intro shows its still: older demos are stopped for tiltRev 7
(`introDemo.functions.ts`). **The intro demo needs re-recording on tiltRev 7.**

**Art** (`tiltArt.renderer.ts`, the A4 Toybox look): the screen-fixed oak table with its planks and
lamplight pool sized to the taller world; the disc, its grain, groove, drop shadow and bevelled rim drawn at
each maze's radius and moving with the camera; the disc, its holes, ice and carved strips baked into one
sprite per maze and scale (drawn rotated each frame, as the old reduced-effects path did); frosted ice cells
with glints; brass and verdigris keys with a soft glow (light only); gate bars with a keyhole plate that
swing open and fade (the old padlock gate's swing); quick stars as the gold star with a draining ring; the
marbles with their screen-fixed shadow and gloss. The HUD keeps "Maze n / 3" and "Stars a / b" and adds the
quick stars left with the window's ring. The success layer (`TILT_X_FX`) keeps tiltRev 6's moments in order
(the replay moments read them by index) and adds QUICK STAR! and GATE OPEN! pops, each at the marble's place
under the last camera drawn. Reduced motion: the camera eases faster (no long drift), the key and gate
animations snap. Reduced effects: no gloss, no key glow, the strips' lite pass. Arcade bounds: everything
that collides (walls, gates, holes, cups) is drawn on its sim edges; keys and stars are pickups, drawn
within their pickup square.

**Sound** (no new files or manifest entries): a quick star plays Tilt's gold-star chime a little higher
(`tilt.starTaken`, rate 1.25), a key plays the gate latch (`tilt.gate`), in `tiltSounds` (`gameSounds.ts`).
