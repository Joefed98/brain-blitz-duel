# The arcade 200 expansions (26 Oct 2026): Hover and Grappler

Four parked arcade expansions were finished under the agreed arcade rule (below), each given one new precision
feature and measured against the tuning bar: Hover (from #165), Grappler and Roofbound (from #164) and Scrumper
(from #163). **Hover (`hoverRev 9`) and Grappler (`swingRev 13`) meet the bar and ship.** Roofbound and Scrumper
were built and proved the same way but miss the bar, so, as Joe asked, they are left out rather than forcing the
curve (their numbers are below). Per-game notes: `x200-hover.md`, `x200-swing.md`.

## The arcade rule (agreed with Joe, 26 Oct)

Every player in a room plays the identical run, so seed luck is not a fairness problem. Two things hold:

1. **Guaranteed opportunities**: every run, whatever the seed, holds enough scoring items and feature chances that
   perfect play reaches 200; the seed decides where and when, never how many. Each game's check proves the
   perfect-play ceiling ≥ 200 on every tuning seed, every fresh seed and every room-bank course.
2. **Skill headroom**: better play always earns more, compared on the same seeds. Features pay a modest fixed
   amount on a rising scale, on top of base scoring, and reach full value only at elite pace. Every feature and
   pickup appears at seeded moments and places, never from how the player has played.
   `arcade-room-schedule-check` proves two players in the same room get identical spawns, pickups, par times and
   schedules whatever they do: every room deal of each shipped game is played idle, at random and by the weak, good
   and elite players, and every scheduled item (`scripts/x200/<type>.ts`) must read the same in every run.

## Tools

- `scripts/arcade-skill-bands/human.ts`: tiers `weak` and `elite` beside the four levels (`TIERS`; a tier plays
  as its level, `TIER_LEVEL`, with its own profile and random stream, so elite never plays near-perfect's run;
  bots read `human.tier`). `arcade-skill-bands.ts`: `--levels=weak,…,elite,random` (`random`: the bot module's
  `randomBot`), `--from=N` (fresh seeds). Session A's "elite level" PR for the tool was not on main when this was
  built; this is a minimal stand-in, so the final tuning should be re-measured with Session A's bots once that
  lands (assumption).
- Seed sets: tuning = indices 0–59 (`--seeds=60`), fresh = 10000–10199 (`--seeds=200 --from=10000`), never tuned on.

## Why two games are left out

- **Roofbound** (`flipRev 8`, sweet spots; built on a local branch, not in this PR): the tuning seeds meet every
  target, but on the fresh seeds near-perfect's median is 163 (bar 165–175). A second fit (flatter top, steeper
  pay) moved it from 162 / 187 to 163 / 185; its median moves with how many of the ten gold stars a seed set
  allows on the steep top of the base curve (flipRev 6 shows the same: good 120 tuning, 100 fresh).
- **Scrumper** (`snipRev 11`, par time per level): near-perfect's p90 is 187 on both sets and elite's tuning median
  171. 45 scoring structures were tried on the tuning seeds; near-perfect's top-decile runs beat elite's median run
  on every scored feature (levels, units, speed, golden apples, leaves, par cuts, par time), because perfect-level
  timing windows are 1–6 ticks wide, so no strictly rising curve separates them. It needs course design (roomier
  windows) or a different precision model, not pay.

Both are fully built (sim, art, feature, ceiling proofs at 200 on all 260 seeds, room modules, checks) on local
branches; ask the session to push them as parked branches if wanted.
