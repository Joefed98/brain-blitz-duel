Six rules every screen follows
  • One gold button a screen. Gold means "this plays". Everything else is navy.
  • One hero a page. The cut-corner card. Everything under it is flat panels and rows.
  • Colour means something. A family, a level, a win or a loss. Never decoration.
  • Numbers are the display type. Ratings, scores and coins in the wide 900.
  • Fewer words. A title, one line, a number. Detail sits behind a tap.
  • Dark frame, bright game. No glow on pages; the light is inside the play area.
