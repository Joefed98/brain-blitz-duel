# Badge shapes for the Blender enamel pins (S5, proposal for Joe's OK)

Joe's four shapes (27 Nov 2026): **round** for skill, **hexagon** for streaks, **shield** for competition, **star** for
collection. Today's six groups (docs/art/badges) map onto them below. Rarity still sets the metal (Common bronze, Rare
silver, Epic gold, Elite black nickel, Legendary platinum); slugs, names and rules are unchanged. Nothing is rendered
until Joe approves this list.

Counts: Hexagon (streaks) 6, Round (skill) 16, Shield (competition) 21, Star (collection) 11.

Judgment calls (marked † in the table):

- The eleven category masters and Grand Master go **round**: three stars in every game is a skill feat, though it could read as collecting stars.
- Arena Ace (8 wins in a row in one Arena) goes **hexagon**: it is a streak, though won in a tournament.
- Serial Winner (10 tournaments, not in a row) and Photo Finish (a 1-point win) go **shield**.
- All-Rounder (a star in every game) goes **star**: it counts things gathered.
- Friendly, Party Host and Founding Player have no shape of their own, so they join **star** as collection and special pieces.
