# Expansion E5: Swish, Flightpath and Slice to 200

25 Oct 2026. World expansion and depth for the three fairground and kitchen arcade games (gameplay
change). Each game gets a bigger world that fills the iPhone 11 play screen, its agreed skill
features in its polished art, and a new version stamp scored to 200 through `ROUND_MAX`
(docs/scoring-200.md). Older stamps replay and score exactly as before.

| Game | New stamp | World (was) | ROUND_MAX |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swish (`bintoss`) | `binRev 7` | 430 × 728 (390 × 560) | 200 |
| Flightpath (`bullseye`) | `bullRev 7` | 390 × 728 (390 × 560) | 200 |
| Slice (`slice`) | `sliceRev 9` | 430 × 805 (390 × 730) | 200 |

Rooms, practice, daily and challenges deal the new stamps (`arcade.server.ts`). `LIVE_BANDS` keeps each
game's band-3 revision and curve (stored rounds and the score-band checks use them) and adds
`dealt`, the stamp rooms deal now (`liveDealtRev`).

## Swish, binRev 7

- **More world.** A wider, taller fairground (430 × 728): the extra 168 px is sky above the original
  top, so every original coordinate (ball, floor, rim, buckets, hoops, spinners) stays where it was; the
  world's right edge moves out to 430. A drag throws 1.2 × as fast (`BINTOSS_V7_REF_V`) so the back of the
  stall is in reach; the aim arc shows the stronger throw.
- **Far bins score more.** On throws 1–6 a narrow far bucket (`BINTOSS_FAR_HALF`, the narrowed rev-2
  bucket) stands still 110–140 px behind the main bucket (seeded per throw, never past the stall's right
  side, always more than a ball's width clear of the main bucket). Either bucket is a basket; the far one
  adds 9 far points (`BINTOSS_FAR_POINTS`, up to 50). It has its own rim and walls (bounces and wobble as
  the main bucket's) and a gold "+9" pennant.
- **Kept.** The main bucket's distances, narrowing, the moving bucket (throws 7–10), shifting wind and the
  spinners are binRev 6's. The bonus hoops (three of throws 2–5) now hang over the far bucket, each placed
  on a reference throw a drag can actually make (`BINTOSS_V7_MAX_SPEED`).
- **Score.** binRev 6's units (10 a bucket, 3 a hoop swish) on `BINTOSS_V7_BAND_CURVE` to 140, 10 for all
  ten in, plus the far points: 200 needs all ten in, every hoop and the far bucket on (nearly) every throw
  that has one.
- **Art.** The far bucket is the same tin bucket art with its contact shadow; the "FAR n · +k" line sits
  under the throw line; a far basket's pip has a white ring; the landing animation drops into the far bucket.
- **Sound.** `shared.celebrate.chime` on a far basket.

## Flightpath, bullRev 7

- **More world.** A taller range (390 × 728; 168 px above the original top). Each dart's board stands at
  one of five distances (`BULLSEYE_V7_SCALES` / `_HEIGHTS`): near boards low and big (104 %), far ones high
  and small (64 %), height jittered ±10 px, darts 1–3 from the three nearest. A drag carries the dart 1.5 ×
  as far (`BULLSEYE_V7_FLIGHT_NUM`), so every board's centre is in reach. The drifting board's swing is held
  inside the range for the big near boards. Each board stands on a wooden post down to the counter.
- **Bullseye streak multiplier.** Darts in the gold 10 in a row (wherever the 10 sits; the inner bull
  included) build a streak ×n; from ×3 each bull adds 2 × (n − 2) streak points (+2, +4, +6 …), up to 50.
  Any other dart ends the streak. The HUD shows "STREAK ×n +k"; a streak bull's landing pop adds "×n +k".
- **Kept.** Wind, sway, the moving board, ring shuffles, the inner bull and the value balloons (placed for
  each dart's own board) are bullRev 6's. The shared layer's "n BULLS IN A ROW" banner still fires.
- **Score.** The dart total on bullRev 6's strict curve (`BULLSEYE_V7_BAND_CURVE`, the same knots) to
  150, plus the streak points.
- **Sound.** `shared.celebrate.zap` for each streak bull that earns points, louder up the streak.

## Slice, sliceRev 9

- **More world.** A larger board (430 × 805, the same shape as the taller board, so it fills the screen at
  a slightly smaller scale) and more throws (up to five a window, was four).
- **Food sizes.** Bread (the bagel) big (26 px) and worth 1, cabbage and meat 20 px and worth 2, onions
  small (14 px) and worth 3, golden fruit tiny (10 px) and worth 5. A slice credits the food's value ×
  the combo multiplier; combos still qualify on three foods in one path; the share is out of the most the
  run offers (every food at ×3). Chillies and bottles keep their size.
- **Golden fruit.** Six tiny, fast golden fruits a run (never in the first 5 s). One cut while a combo is
  live adds 3 at ×2 or 6 at ×3 (`SLICE_V9_GOLD_POINTS`).
- **Order tickets.** Five tickets, one at a time (8 s each from 8 s): each asks for one food 1–3 times in a
  row (cutting another plain food starts the count again; golden fruit, bottles and chillies don't); filling
  one adds 5. Each ticket's food is thrown at least its count (and one spare where the window allows) in its
  window. The ticket card sits at the board's top right: the food, "×n IN A ROW", pips and the time left.
- **Dodge streak.** A cutting swipe that passes within 40 px of a chilli's skin without cutting it marks
  it; when it then leaves the board uncut it is a dodge. The n-th dodge in a row adds 2, 4, then 6. Cutting
  a chilli ends the streak (and still costs 10 and the combo).
- **Bonus.** Golden fruit, tickets and dodges add up to 50 (`SLICE_V9_BONUS_CAP`), shown as "BONUS +n
  DODGE ×n" under the combo tag; a golden fruit cut pops "+n GOLD".
- **Kept.** The multi-slice combo, the blade, the long path, the three bottles and the chilli penalty.
- **Score.** The value-weighted share on `SLICE_V9_BAND_CURVE` to 150 (fitted), plus the bonus, less 10 a
  chilli.
- **Sound.** `shared.celebrate.chime` for golden fruit, `complete` for a filled ticket,
  `shared.celebrate.crackle1` for a dodge (all through `playSound`: mute and the Effects slider apply).

### Merge retune (26 Oct 2026; binRev 7 and sliceRev 9 are not live, so the stamps are kept)

Measured on 400 seeds: the 200 tuning seeds plus 200 fresh ones (`bun scripts/arcade-skill-bands.ts bintoss bullseye slice --rev=x --seeds=400`).

- **What failed:** near-perfect sat at 151 (Swish) and 145 (Slice), with Slice's top 10 % at 188.
- **What changed:** both base curves now lift near-perfect's play, with each segment at least a third of its old slope. The feature points are paid as before.
  - Swish: 83 → 139, 97 → 140, 109 → 142.
  - Slice: 643 → 72, 704 → 132, 743 → 135, 797 → 136, 850 → 150.
- **The cost:** their tops are compressed. Above near-perfect's typical play, more units add only a few points (Swish 83–109 units, Slice 704–797), and the full 200 needs the feature bonus at its best.
- **Fixtures:** the expansion-e5 new-stamp runs were re-recorded, and the two new-stamp runs in replay-runs.json were re-scored.

Median / top 10 % / best (old median in brackets). No run at any level scores 0.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Swish | tuning (200) | 80 (80) | 98 (105) | 138 (128) | 166 / 184 / 200 |
| Swish | fresh (200) | 77 (83) | 96 (100) | 133 (128) | 166 / 184 / 200 |
| Slice | tuning (200) | 70 (68) | 107 (101) | 168 (130) | 168 / 185 / 200 |

**Flightpath is not retuned here.** Near-perfect's top 10 % already plays the most streak points a run can earn (72) on a near-full base (146). No payout keeps that top 10 % under about 185 while elite play can still reach 200, so this needs a design decision (see the PR).

## Assumptions

- Swish's "bins at different distances": the main bucket keeps its distance progression and a far bucket
  stands further back on throws 1–6 (never on the moving-bucket throws 7–10, where the main bucket's slide
  fills the stall's width). The hoops moved over the far bucket so the extra 50 needs hoops and far shots
  together.
- Flightpath's "targets at different distances and heights": distance is drawn as size and height on the
  range (nearer = lower and bigger); the streak counts the gold 10 wherever it sits on shuffled darts.
- Slice's ticket is "in a row" so it is a choice (cutting other food resets it), and golden fruit only pays
  inside a combo, so both reward skill rather than slicing everything; the dodge needs a close pass, not
  only an uncut chilli.
- The world grows by translation (Swish and Flightpath) so the original sims' coordinates, and therefore
  every older stamp, are untouched; Slice's larger board is a new schedule builder on the same physics.
- The review screenshots (`screenshots/expansion-e5/`, `bun scripts/shots/e5-shots.ts`) are the games'
  own renderers on a bot's mid-run state, placed as the play screen places them; the header is a stand-in.
