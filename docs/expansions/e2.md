# Expansion E2: Meteor, Fission and Tempo to 200

25 Oct 2026. Gameplay change, one PR (session E2 of the eight parallel expansions). Each game gets more
world, the agreed skill features in its Polish A1 art style, and a maximum of 200 through `ROUND_MAX`
(docs/scoring-200.md). New rounds deal the new stamps; every older stamp replays and scores exactly as
before (expansion-e2-check, 108 runs recorded on unmodified main, plus the games' existing legacy checks).

| Game | New stamp | Before | Run | World |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meteor | `snakeRev 9` | `snakeRev 8` | 60 s | 468 × 868 (19 × 34 squares; was 390 × 724, 15 × 28) |
| Fission | `burstRev 7` | `burstRev 6` | 90 s | 390 × 650 (ceiling from 24 px, was 40; higher bounces) |
| Tempo | `beatRev 9` | `beatRev 8` | 75 s (was 60) | 390 × 730, four lanes then five |

## Scoring shape (all three)

Score = the game's usual achievement on a new base curve to 150 (fitted so a player who ignores the new
features lands where they did before) + the features' points, each capped, 50 in all, to 200
(`bandTotal(parts, acted, 200)`; no input still scores 0). The reveal's breakdown shows the base line and a
"Skill bonus (…)" line with each feature's points, and both add up to the stored score. Old revisions keep
their own curves and the 150 clamp.

## Meteor (`snakeRev 9`; `src/lib/arcade/snake.ts`, `snake.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/snake.tsx`)

- **More world:** a 19 × 34 field (646 squares, was 420) in a 468 × 868 world, the same 24 px squares and
  art, scaled to fit the arena (414 px wide on an iPhone 11, 21 px squares). The star queue, rocks
  (snakeRev 6's pace) and the 20-star shower are drawn exactly as before over the bigger grid
  (`init`, `rockSchedule` and `initRev3` take the column count; 15 by default, so older rounds are unchanged).
- **Wormholes:** two pairs of mouths (teal and magenta), one pair top ↔ bottom and one left ↔ right, 2+
  squares off the border, never on a star, rock, shower square or lane. Stepping into a mouth comes
  straight out of its twin, still heading the same way (`snakeTarget`). A star eaten within 1 s of a trip
  is a warp star: +2 (up to 8).
- **Gold comet star:** from 8 s, about every 7 s (±1 s) a gold star appears on a fixed square for 3.5 s,
  fading (a gold diamond behind the star sprite, a shrinking timer ring, a blink in its last third). Eating
  it is worth 1–5, more the sooner (`snakeGoldWorth`), up to 20. The meteor doesn't grow.
- **Speed lanes:** one row and one column (violet, chevrons drifting both ways). Travelling along a lane,
  each step is 3 ticks faster (never under 3, `snakeStepTicksFor`). From a ride's sixth square, every 4
  squares score 1 (up to 8).
- **Combo:** stars within 1.25 s of each other raise the combo (HUD "COMBO x n"); from the third, each star
  adds 1–3 (up to 14).
- **Score:** stars on `SNAKE_V9_BAND_CURVE` (150 at 31) + gold 20 + combo 14 + lanes 8 + warps 8.
- **Sounds:** gold star → spark chime; warp → zap; combo of 3+ → count ding; every 4 lane squares → tick.

## Fission (`burstRev 7`; `src/lib/arcade/burst.ts`, `burst.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/burst.tsx`)

- **More world:** the world already fills its arena box above the 76 px arrows (414 × 688 on an iPhone 11,
  390 × 650 scaled 1.06), so the arena grows upward inside it: the ceiling starts at 24 px (the HUD is one
  slim line), atoms bounce to 84 / 70 / 54 / 38 % of the arena (was 75 / 60 / 45 / 30) and the ceiling never
  comes lower than 70 % of the arena above the floor (was 60 %), so atoms use the top of the screen.
  Assumption: shrinking the arrows to make the world taller was ruled out (their size is a tested control).
- **Chain reactions:** a split atom (not the smallest, which just pops) gives off an orange shockwave ring
  growing 4 px a tick to 60 / 50 / 40 px by size. Any atom it touches splits too and sends its own (white)
  ring, up to 3 links; fragments under 20 ticks old shrug rings off. From the second link, link n adds
  min(2n, 6), up to 22. HUD pop "CHAIN x n".
- **Power-ups:** the DOUBLE beam (a green two-beam icon from 1 in 4 purple atoms, in place of half the
  BURST drops): 8 s of two beams 14 px either side of the ship. FREEZE (existing) now drops from 1 in 8
  green atoms (was 1 in 12), and every 2 atoms hit while frozen add 1 (up to 6).
- **Golden atom:** from 10 s, every 12 s a small golden atom (18 px) crosses the top of the arena (its
  side from a seed stream of its own); a beam that reaches it takes it for 4 (up to 16). It never splits
  and can't hurt the ship.
- **Wave-clear time bonus:** 3 for clearing a wave within 5 s of it appearing, 2 within 8 s, 1 within
  12 s (up to 6). Pop "FAST +n".
- **Score:** atoms hit less 5 a life on `BURST_V7_BAND_CURVE` (to 140) + 10 for lasting the 90 s + chains
  22 + golden 16 + fast waves 6 + frozen hits 6.
- **Sounds:** chain link → meter crackles (pitched up with the link); DOUBLE caught → zap; golden atom →
  spark chime; fast wave → count ding.

## Tempo (`beatRev 9`; `src/lib/arcade/beat.ts`, `beat.renderer.ts`, `src/puzzles/beat.tsx`)

- **More world:** a 75-second song (was 60) built from the seed on its own stream in sections at 120 BPM:
  quarter notes, then eighths, then chords and holds; at 40 s, after a one-second break with no notes
  ("5 LANES!"), the lanes become five (a hot-pink fifth lane, 78 px each across the same 390 px), and the
  last section (56–75 s) is the busiest. About 210 notes (beatRev 8: about 150 in 60 s).
- **Fever:** Perfects (2) and Goods (1) fill the meter in the HUD strip (60); a miss empties it. Full, it
  flashes TAP FEVER!; a tap on the strip (input kind 12, `BEAT_INPUT_FEVER`) starts 8 s of fever: the lanes
  wash gold and pink and every note hit counts again (a point per 8 quarters, up to 26).
- **Golden notes:** about one plain tap in ten, white-hot in a gold frame with a pixel star; hit on the very
  tick it meets the line for +1 (up to 8).
- **Double-tap notes:** two notes 9 ticks apart in one lane, marked with white pips and tied by a bridge;
  both Perfect adds 1 (up to 8).
- **Perfect streak:** a point per 10 Perfects in the run's longest streak (up to 8).
- **Score:** per mille of the chart (Perfect 4, Good 3, OK 1, miss −2; both halves of a double count) on
  `BEAT_V9_BAND_CURVE` (150 for every note Perfect) + fever 26 + golden 8 + doubles 8 + streak 8.
- **The longer song's music:** the recorded beat track is 60 s. From about 2.5 s before it ends, the
  synthesised beat (`playBeatStep`, the track's own fallback) carries the last section on the same grid. No
  new audio file was added (see Shared requests).
- **The clock:** Tempo's `timeLimitMs` is per game type, so every Tempo round now shows a 75 s clock. Rounds
  dealt before this deploys (a room or a day's Daily Arcade still in play) run their 60 s and end; their
  clock jumps from 15 s to 0 when the run ends. Practice and new rooms always deal beatRev 9.
- **Sounds:** fever start → zap; golden note → spark chime; double tap → tick; each 10 in the Perfect
  streak → count ding.

## Intro, help and demos

Each game's intro has one or two short new lines (`instructions.ts`), the help a line per feature, and the
intro SCORE line (`introScore.ts`, ≤ 90 characters) names the features with `{max}`. The intro stills (the
SVG `IntroExample`) gain a wormhole, lane and gold star (Meteor), a shockwave and golden atom (Fission) and a
golden note and the fever meter (Tempo), same size. **The three intro demos are out of date and need
re-recording on the new stamps:** `getIntroDemoFn` now plays a demo only when it was recorded on snakeRev 9,
burstRev 7 or beatRev 9, so until then each intro shows its still.

## Accessibility and performance

Mute, the Effects slider and the intro-demo hush apply to every new sound (`playCueSlot` through
`src/lib/fx/expansionSounds.ts`). Reduced motion: no drifting chevrons, ring growth, sparkles or rising
pops (still rings and markers instead); reduced effects: no glows (the existing `glow()` rule) and no
shockwave band. New art is a few rects and cached sprites a frame; no shadowBlur at draw time.

## Score distributions (old versus new)

Skill-band bots (scripts/arcade-skill-bands/, four levels), through the server's encode + replay path,
`bun scripts/expansions-e2-bands.ts --seeds=30`. Each cell is median / top 10 % / best of 30 runs.
"Today's play" is the new revision with the bot ignoring the new features (for Tempo, whose features are
passive, only fever is skipped and golden notes get no extra care): the target is within about ±10 % of
"old". "Using the features" is the bot playing them at its level.

### Tempo (beatRev 8 → 9)

| Level | Old (median / top 10 % / best) | New, today's play | New, using the features |
|---|---|---|---|
| average | 70 / 80 / 91 | 69 / 81 / 83 | 71 / 83 / 93 |
| good | 100 / 111 / 114 | 106 / 111 / 120 | 112 / 128 / 135 |
| great | 120 / 132 / 145 | 126 / 132 / 138 | 151 / 160 / 167 |
| near | 140 / 150 / 150 | 146 / 162 / 173 | 173 / 188 / 200 |

Reading it: a solid player who ignores the features (the "today's play" medians) lands within about ±10 % of
before in every row but Meteor's average (+11 %) and Fission's near-perfect bot, whose old median (109) sits
below its own great bot's (130) on burstRev 6 (that bot's quirk; its old top 10 % is 150). Using the features
lifts strong players: the best near-perfect runs reach 189 (Meteor), 184–186 (Fission) and 200 (Tempo);
the bots never quite take every feature point, so a strong human using all of them is what reaches 190–200.
Fission's chain reactions and Tempo's golden notes, double taps and Perfect streak reward accuracy as such,
so strong players earn some of those points without aiming for them (Fission "today's play" top 10 % 162–170).

## Shared requests

- **Tempo's music for the longer song:** a 75 s (or loopable) recording of `music_beat`, or loop support in
  `syncBeatTrack`, so the last 15 s keep the recorded track instead of the synthesised fallback.
- **Per-revision run length:** `puzzleRunMs` reads the game type's `timeLimitMs`; reading a puzzle's own run
  length would let an old 60 s Tempo round keep its 60 s clock.
- **`scoring-targets-revs.ts` / `round-max-check.ts`:** every expansion adds its game to `EXPANSION_STRICT`
  and `RAISED`; one shared list (e.g. from `ROUND_MAX`) would avoid eight PRs touching the same lines.
- **Moments and cues:** the new feature moments draw their own pops and play existing sounds directly; adding
  them to the shared success layer (`arcadeFxGames` events, `MOMENT_TABLE`, `momentCues`) would give them
  replay moments and named cues.
