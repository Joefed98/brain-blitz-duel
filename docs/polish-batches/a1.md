# Polish batch A1: arcade worlds Meteor, Fission, Tempo, Rally, Breaker (25 Oct 2026)

Display only. No change to scoring, rules, timing, difficulty, seeds, hitboxes, physics, server calls,
data, routes, storage keys or version stamps; `bun scripts/practice-stamps.ts` writes an identical
`practiceStamps.data.ts`. World sizes and playfields are today's. Every game keeps its Retro style
(pixel sprites, scanlines, Press Start 2P) and its in-world HUD line.

## Files

- `src/lib/arcade/polishA1.renderer.ts` (new, imported only by the five renderers below): `backdrop`
  (a still scene painted once per world size into a 2× layer, one blit a frame; a resize allocates
  nothing), `glow` (a cached soft radial light, skipped under reduced effects), `addPop` / `drawPops`
  (pixel-font pops outlined in near-black, no shadowBlur), `addShatter` (pixel bursts blitted from
  cached squares, live total capped at `FX_PARTICLE_LIMIT`, none under reduced motion or reduced

- `snake.renderer.ts`, `burst.renderer.ts`, `beat.renderer.ts`, `rally.renderer.ts`,
  `breaker.renderer.ts`, and `src/puzzles/burst.tsx` (the arrows' look only).

  the beam has a soft green halo; a split gives a pixel shatter and "SPLIT!" (the smallest atom: the

  76 px boxes and 16 px gap (tuning-b and tuning-f check the classes); "slightly larger" is the glyph,
  30 → 36 px, with a soft gold glow and text glow.
- **Tempo**: lanes, colours, notes and the white hit line kept. A purple synthwave sky with a magenta
  horizon glow on the hit line, each lane's tint brighter towards the bottom with its own glow rising
  from the line, glowing inner dividers, a lane-coloured marker per lane on the hit line, notes with a
  bright top edge, a PERFECT burst (rays and a flash) in the lane's colour kept inside the lane (sim

- **Rally**: court, brackets, centre line and circle, pads, paddle and HUD kept. A deep blue glow behind

  the paddle and ball glow.

## Assumptions

- "+10" (Meteor, Breaker) is the requested label; it is a celebration, not the stored score (arcade
  scores come from the band curves).
- The Effects slider is the sound effects channel; this batch plays no sound, so it is untouched.
  Reduced motion: pops stand still, no shatters, no PERFECT rays, warnings and glows still. Reduced
  effects: no glows, shatters or beam halo.
- The screen fills (`arenaFills.renderer.ts`) are shared and unchanged; each world's edges stay as dark
  as before, so arcade-fill-check finds no seam.
